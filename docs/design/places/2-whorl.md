# 2. The Whorl (the spiral)

Design 2 for `BRIEF.md`. Layout: `2-whorl.json`. Score: `node docs/design/places/place-score.mjs docs/design/places/2-whorl.json`,
**25/25 pass**. 44 rooms, 193 screens, 55 links, 12 loops. Abilities: **claws**, then **glide**. Boss: the Weaver.

## The place
The bottom of the wood is one dead giant beech. Its fallen limbs and roots coil out from its foot, so the whole wood
floor turns around it in a slow spiral: the Whorl. Wing hatches in a crack at its foot. Inside the trunk is **the
Nave**, a cathedral of heartwood ribs five screens tall, lit by one knothole high in the vault, **the Rose**, with the
real moon in it. Under the Rose the firefly has hung its first and biggest collection, a chandelier of wicks and
lanterns on root chains, and the moths in the Nave circle that instead of the moon.

Around the trunk: west, **the Rootfall** pours down the beech's side into **the Sump**, black water under the roots,
and the **West Chimney** (a hollow sapling) climbs back up to **the Triforium**, a gallery along the outside of the
Nave's west wall. Under the start: **the Undercroft** and **the Moonwell**, the deepest point, where moonlight falls
straight down a crack. East: **the Gulf**, a sinkhole of thorns, and out of its far side rises **the Steeple**, a
lightning-struck hollow pine nine screens tall. The Weaver has strung its web across the Steeple's open top round the
firefly's lamp-heart. That glow over the canopy is the **landmark**, seen through the hatching crack at second 10, from
the Lookout, from the Gulf's edge, and the whole way along the High Canopy.

## The hub: the Nave
A 4 × 5 screen cathedral directly above the Hatching (1 room from the start), with **seven ways**: the Hatching
(below), the Rootfall (west door), the Triforium ledge (west, high: claws), the Rood Loft (up the ribs: claws), the
East Stair (east door), the East Gallery's broken limb across the vault (glide), the Rose (glide). Four of the seven
are locked on the first visit. The thread crosses it **five times** (the scorer's route six), each from a new side or
height, and **it grows on every return**:

| pass | min | she enters | what has changed |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:30 | from the floor (the Hatching) | dark; the chandelier burning under the Rose, ~30 moths circling it; a juvenile beetle on the floor, one wick |
| 2 | 7:30 | high from the west, off the Triforium: the first look *down* into it | the moths she freed in the west rest on the ribs in rows of glinting eyes; a dangler has dropped from the chandelier over the bench |
| 3 | 8:30 | down from the Rood Loft onto the chandelier's chain | she flings across the vault off it; the Hatching's floor knot below now has a lit cup |
| 4–5 | 14:30 | from the east door, after glide | the firefly has hung a second ring on the chandelier and a hawker hunts the vault. She glides the vault, snuffs the outer ring (a 25-moth cloud) and rises to the Rose |
| after | 24+ | down through the Rose from the High Canopy | the chandelier is dark; moonlight falls to the floor; every moth she freed covers the ribs |

## The thread (≈ 24 min for a first-timer; 17 of 44 rooms, 39%)
It circles the start: **west and down, back over the top, through the floor and under, east and up the tower.**

1. **0:00 Hatching → 0:30 Nave.** Control by 0:10; the floor knot (two empty cups) is under her feet.
2. **1:30 Rootfall → 2:30 Sump.** Down the slick roots and over the edge: a one-way drop into black water.
3. **4:00 Husk Hollow: CLAWS**, at the far low end of the west. The Sump's only way out is the chimney, one kick away.
4. **4:30 West Chimney** (8 tall; **the Drip**, moon-drop 1) **→ 6:00 Triforium**, high over the start's row.
5. **7:30 Nave** from above → **8:30 Rood Loft** (moon-drop 2) → **9:30 the floor knot opens** (2 cups).
6. **10:00 Undercroft → 10:30 Moonwell: GLIDE**, straight under the start. Left only by gliding.
7. **11:00 the Gulf** (the first big glide) → **12:30 East Stair → the Weave** (moon-drop 3).
8. **14:30 Nave** from the east → **15:30 the Rose** (moon-drop 4).
9. **17:00 the Gulf → the Steeple's door of four cups.** **17:30–20:30 the Steeple**: the firefly flees up ahead,
   relighting wicks as it goes, and she snuffs them on the climb; the Resin Nook bench below the top.
10. **20:30–23:30 the Weaver** in the Crown → **24:00 the Canopy Edge**, every freed moth spiralling up past her.

Six moon-drops exist; any two open the floor knot and any four the Steeple door (doors count drops held; none are
spent). Three are reachable as soon as she has claws, so the order is hers. Everything else (27 rooms) is off the thread: ~18 min more, ~42 min for all of it.

## What each ability reopens
- **Before claws** the Whorl is a **tree**: 12 rooms, no loops, **12 locked ways in view** (the floor knot, the
  sibling crack, the Rood Loft, the Triforium ledge, the East Gallery limb, the Rose, the chimney's thorn foot from two
  sides, the Wick Garden's ledge, the Weave's mouth, the Gulf, the Old Burrow's pit).
- **Claws** (15 locks, 12 → 20 rooms): the West Chimney and its five doors (Drip, Thorn Flue, Beetle Run end,
  Triforium, Lookout), the Rood Loft, the Triforium ledge and the Wick Garden's ledge below it, the sibling husks, the
  Moth-stone crawl, the Weave, then
  later the Hawk's Notch, the Grotto and the Cache. The first loop closes at once: Nave, Rootfall, Sump, Chimney,
  Triforium, Nave.
- **Glide** (7 locks, all seen before): the Gulf from the stair and from the Undercroft, the Rose, the East Gallery
  limb, the Old Burrow's black pit (into the dark ring under everything), the Lookout's glides to the Far Bough and
  the Canopy Road. It turns the tree into a web: the canopy ring over the top, the dark ring underneath.
- **The keys and the boss** open shortcuts that all lead home to the Nave: the Steeple's side door to the East
  Gallery, the Sap Cup through the Weave, and the Crown's silk to the High Canopy and down through the Rose.

## The best moments
1. **Second 10.** Through the hatching crack: the Steeple's false light over the canopy. Under her feet: a knot with
   two empty cups. The goal and a lock before she has taken a step.
2. **The Rootfall drop.** She goes over the edge of slick roots into black water with no way back. The claws are
   one room away, and she finds them by looking.
3. **Over the top.** Kicking up the chimney, running the Triforium high above where she hatched, then stepping off its
   ledge into the Nave from above for the first time, her own moths on the ribs below.
4. **Through the floor.** The knot fills and gives. She falls under her own hatching, into the Moonwell's shaft of real
   moonlight, and then makes her first glide over the Gulf with the Steeple ahead.
5. **The vault and the tower.** She glides off the chandelier, snuffs its ring and rises into the Rose. Then she climbs
   nine screens of hollow pine after the fleeing firefly to the Weaver's web.

## Dead ends (15, 34%) and what each pays
Sibling Husks (heart-seed) · Rood Loft (moon-drop) · Husk Hollow (claws) · Moonwell (glide) · Drip (moon-drop) ·
Beetle Warren (fight + heart-seed) · Thorn Flue (challenge + glow shard) · Far Bough (glow shard) · Hawk's Notch
(glow shard) · Moss Bed (bench) · Glow-worm Grotto (bench) · Resin Nook (bench) · Hawkers' Ring (challenge +
heart-seed) · Firefly's Cache (glow shard) · Canopy Edge (the exit). Four heart-seeds and five glow shards in all;
three benches are quiet dead ends, so she chooses when to rest.

## Secrets (one screen, one problem)
- **The Moth-stone crawl**: a draught moves the moss on the stone's back wall. With claws it is a crawl to the
  Rootfall's top.
- **The Drowned Cup**: only seen across the black pit after the old burrback is beaten.
- **The Thorn Cradle**: moths flutter up out of one gap in the Gulf's thorn floor. A slow glide drops into it.
- **The Comb**: wick-light glows through a slot in the East Gallery's bark, and the slot drops into the Weave's top.
- **The Firefly's Cache**: behind the Briar Walk's last briar, its signal scratched on the wall.
- **The cricket's map** needs 30 freed moths. Nothing tells you this; the cricket just waits.

## Biggest risk
**The Nave carries the design.** The thread crosses it five times. If it doesn't visibly change each time, the spiral
reads as "back to the same big room again", which is exactly the monotony Matt played. It is also the biggest single
space (20 screens) to paint, and it must read as one cathedral, not as a wall of ribs. The mitigation is built into the
layout: every pass comes from a new side and height (the floor, the west ledge, the chandelier's chain, the east door,
the Rose), and the room's state changes on each one. If the build can't afford those state changes, cut the Nave to 4 × 4
and move the dangler and hawker changes to the Hatching and the East Stair, before cutting passes.
**Second risk:** the numbers have little margin. The thread is 39% (one more room on it fails), and claws opens 12 → 20
(1.67×). Build changes must keep new rooms off the thread.
