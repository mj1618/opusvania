# 3. The Lampweb: four quarters round a web

Design 3 for `BRIEF.md` (2026-10-20). Layout: `3-lampweb.json`. Spine: **quadrants around a centre**, as in Animal
Well, mixed with Hollow Knight's Crossroads. `place-score.mjs`: **27 / 27 pass**.

## The place
The wood floor is a bowl, and four old trees stand round a clearing at its bottom. Between them the Weaver has strung
**the Lampweb**, a great orb web around the firefly's brightest lamp. Thousands of moths circle the lamp, and dead
ones hang in the strands. The web is moored by four silver lines, and each one runs off to a far corner of the wood,
where it is tied at a lamp. The web and its glare hide the moon from the whole wood.

**Wing's want:** cut the four lines, bring the web down, and see the moon again. From the hatching branch she sees all
four lines run off into the dark. She knows the goals from the first minute, but not the routes (Animal Well's four
flames). Each line she cuts slackens one quarter of the web. Real moonlight then falls where she stands, and that is
where her new ability comes from. When the fourth line goes, the web's heart sinks within reach of the clearing,
and the Weaver comes down to defend it.

The four quarters each have their own feel, creature, set piece and goal in the far corner:

| Quarter | Feel | Creatures | Corner goal (gate in) | Gives |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **NW, the Boughs** | wind, sway, height, falls onto lower limbs | hawkers on lamp orbits | the Old Pine: the bough snaps behind her (open, one way) | **glide** |

Each corner is a different set piece, and you leave each ability room only with the ability it gives: the glide off
the pine, the climb up the taproot, the double jump out of the sack.

## The hub: the clearing under the web
It is a 5×4-screen set piece, the biggest space by far, and it has nine ways out. The fall comes in from above
(claws to climb back up). The bough road and the root gate lead west, the silk arch and the cap steps east. The heart's
door is overhead (keys:4: the area's last door, with a bench under it, as with the Black Egg Temple). The moth-stone
(the map) and Tatter's roost are next door, and the drain grate opens from below. Three of its exits are locked at
first: the fall, the heart and the drain. Every quarter's halls look into it through a gap in the trees, so the web's
glow is the thing you steer by.

**It changes on every return.** Each cut line sags one quarter of the web: real moonlight lands on that side of the
floor, slack strands hang down into reach (to cut, to be grabbed by), and danglers climb down them. Tatter counts
the freed moths, as Animal Well's egg room counts eggs. The scored main path crosses the hub 6 times.

## What each ability reopens (all four quarters each time)
- **Glide** (10 locks). It opens the most of any ability: reachable rooms go from 19 to 33.
  - Centre: the promise gap (hatch to mast).
  - NW: the pine's two exits, and the windfall gauntlet (a moth-heart shard).
  - SW: the pit into the den; the galleries' gap into **the deep ring** (drowned roots, then the drain, then the
    grate up into the hub, then the mire).
  - NE: the larder's mouth; the orb gallery (then the shelter bench, then the drip down into the Capwood); the husk
    wall (then the spokes over the heart).
  - SE: the steps' gap into the bounce well.
- **Claws** (12):
  - Centre: the crown (Long Breath), and the fall both ways.
  - NW: the knot-hole (Quick Wings), and the pine to the taproot.
  - SW: the den's climb out, and the crack from either side (Keen Blade).
  - NE: the silk hole (a shard).
  - SE: the root shelf (Night Eyes), the spore ledge (a shard), and the stump's side face (the SE line).
- **Double jump** (7):
  - Centre: the fall up to the spokes (a shard).
  - NW: the hawkers' nest (a shard), and the high twig (a shard).
  - SW: the hoard ledge (a shard).
  - NE: the larder's way out, and the hangings' roof (Sharp Wings).
  - SE: the stump's window (the SE line).

Claws and double jump can be found in either order, and the SE corner takes either one.

## The 5 best moments
1. **The vista.** From the hatching branch: the glowing web, moths circling, four silver lines into the dark. Every
   goal is shown at once, and no route.
2. **The first line.** It whips away across the canopy toward the centre, the NW quarter of the web sags, and the
   moon shows for the first time as her wings dry. The first glide carries her across the whole thorn hollow.
3. **Up the taproot.** She beats the old burrback in the blackest room in the wood, then climbs seven screens with
   claws she has had for thirty seconds, and comes out in the old pine. The world folds: the SW corner is under the NW
   one.
4. **The Larder.** A silk sack full of wrapped moths, and a lamp wrapped in silk. She cuts the line, and the way out is
   straight up, with a jump she didn't have a minute ago.
5. **The web comes down.** She comes back to a clearing gone silver, the web sunk to just over the floor and its heart
   in reach, and the Weaver climbing down to meet her.

## Secrets
- Ten of the 13 pockets that are not rests are one-screen problems that wait for a later ability: the tell (a wick
  by a ledge, a slick face with a glint above), the lock and the key all fit in one view. The other three are the
  map, Tatter and the exit.
- 28 of the 47 rooms (60%) are out of reach until she has an ability. On a first pass they are seen, not reached:
  - the knot from the mast's top lamp;
  - the nest and the high twig from the limbs;
  - the hoard and the crack from the sink;
  - the silk hole and the roof from the hangings;
  - the spore ledge from the seedfall;
  - the root shelf from the steps.
- Bigger secrets:
  - **the deep ring**, a loop under the hub from the Burrows to the Capwood. The drain grate opens into the clearing
    from below;
  - **the spokes**, a walk round the web's rim over the boss arena;
  - **the windfall gauntlet**.

## Loops
There are 14 independent loops (the score counts 17, because it also counts the second link of each one-way pair).
- 40 of 60 links (67%) lie on a loop of 6 rooms or fewer, and the median shortest loop is 5.
- 17 links lie on no loop: the 16 dead-end pockets and the boss door.
- The other 3 links make the 8-room east loop: the Silk, the drip, the Capwood and back.

Each quarter's core is a 3–5-room ring that only closes with that quarter's ability:
- road → mast → limbs → pine → road;
- gate → sink → den → taproot → pine → road → hub.

Cross-links tie the corners together, as Animal Well's wraps do:
- the taproot (SW to NW);
- the drip (NE to SE);
- the deep ring (SW to SE, under the hub);
- the spokes (NE to the fall, over the heart).

## Where it borrows
- **Animal Well:**
  - quarters round a centre, each with its creature, its set piece at the far end and its goal in the corner;
  - the goals shown at once (the four lines, like the map's four flames);
  - finds counted at the hub (Tatter, like the egg room);
  - one screen, one problem;
  - tools that reopen every region;
  - far corners tied together.
- **Hollow Knight's Crossroads:**
  - the drop into the hub (the well, our fall);
  - the area's last door one room from the entry, with a bench in front (the Black Egg Temple, our heart);
  - the map next to the hub (Cornifer, our moth-stone);
  - shortcuts that open into the busiest room (the Shaman pillar, our drain grate);
  - more locks shown than open ways (21 locks seen for 19 open rooms).
- **Super Metroid:** two different locks in the first room (the promise gap and the crown), and the return is
  changed (the hub's light, its strands and its danglers after each cut).
- **Ori:** an ability room that can be left only with that ability, and the climb out is its first test (the
  taproot).

## Biggest risk
**A checklist.** Four errands from a hub ("go to the corner, cut the line, come back", four times) can read as chores,
and four quarters around a symmetric centre can blur together. Against it:
- Every corner is a different set piece and verb: a fall then a glide, a fight then a climb, a glide in then a jump
  out, a stump with two ways in.
- After the first line, the order is the player's choice.
- The cross-links mean she rarely goes home the way she came.
- The hub is visibly changed after every cut.

A smaller risk: nine doors in one 5×4 room could crowd the set piece. The clearing has to be composed so that the web,
not the doors, is what you see.
