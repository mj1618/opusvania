# 4. The Hanging Sun

**Lens:** interlocking loops around a landmark (Super Metroid's Brinstar, Metroid Dread, Dark Souls' Firelink), folded
into 2D. **Length:** ~24.5 min on the critical path with the boss; ~35 min with everything. **Map:** 44 screens on a
10 × 7 grid. **Kit at the hatch:** run, jump, blade, pogo, and the fling (v3's verb). **Found here:** glide (3:00),
claws (13:00), Gust (24:30, the boss's reward).

## Through-line
Wing wants the stragglers back. They are circling **the Hanging Sun**, the firefly's biggest lamp slung in a dead oak
in the middle of the bowl, because they think it is the moon. To put it out she has to get round it to every side.

## Arc
Wonder (the vista: the whole bowl is the map) → curiosity (round the west) → dread (the drowned roots, the only place
the Sun is out of sight) → strength (claws in the first real moonlight) → command (the Skein, looking down on the Sun)
→ homecoming (her own cocoon, the boss's moon patch) → the fight → release (the Sun falls into her hub, and the moon
comes down the trunk).

## The map logic
The bowl has one thing in the middle, and **every room draws it at its true bearing**. Left of it, right of it, under
it, and at last above it: the player always knows where they are relative to the Sun, so it does the job of a map
(the real map is a secret, the moth-stone). Around it are **three loops, one round each side of the oak, and they
cross at the hub and at the places you already know**:
- **West** (5–11 min): down the slope, through the floor into the hub, west through the dark roots, up a glow-cap
  chimney, and out of a knot-hole above the glade where she got her wings. Then the Pine Crook, and the West Lantern.
- **Under-and-east** (11–16 min): from the hub down through the drowned roots under the clearing, up the Moon Well
  (claws), and out onto the Thornbank. The Sun is on her *left* now, and her own Web Wall is across the silk. Then the
  East Lantern. The bolt she cuts in the floor is the hub's locked east gate.
- **Over** (16–21 min): up the Mossback onto the Skein above the crown, down onto the Crown Lantern, and off the
  Skein's end onto the Long Limb, the road the swarm flew at minute 0. It leads to her own cocoon.

The west and east loops both start at the hub and open in **either order**: the player chooses (Firelink). The
third loop needs claws. **The three lanterns are the progress bar:** each one hangs on a frame line of the Sun's web,
so snuffing it cuts the line. The web sags, the Sun tilts and dims, and a cloud of moths lifts past the moon, and all
of it shows from every room. Whichever lantern still burns tells you which side of the Sun to go round next. That is
the wayfinding: no glyphs, no guide moths, no whispers.

## The best moments
1. **"This is where I got my wings."** At 8:00 the chimney's last fling comes out through the dark knot-hole she
   stared up at from the glade. She looks down at the spot where she basked.
2. **The Sun on the wrong side.** At 14:00 she climbs out of the Moon Well onto the Thornbank, sees the Sun on her left,
   and sees her own Web Wall through the silk. She went *under* the clearing. Two minutes later the bolt she cuts drops
   her into her own hub.
3. **Above it.** On the Skein she looks down on the Sun for the first time, with the orbit under her feet and the
   dragonfly passing beneath. Then she drops onto the Crown Lantern and pogoes down its flames.
4. **The fall.** The Sun drops down the hollow oak into the Undersun's pool and goes out with a hiss. She was resting
   under it all along, and now the moon shines down the trunk onto that spot.

## Density on every visit
- **Each room has 2–3+ things within reach:** a wick or a chain to fling, a predator, something to cut (leaves, bark,
  silk, a crust), and a find or a lock you can see.
- **Ecology by radius** sets the density, rising toward the Sun:
  - outer ring: juveniles and beetles eating the dead that fall from the orbit;
  - middle: danglers on the oak's limbs and hawkers hunting the orbit;
  - under: backswimmers in the water where the dead float, and the old burrback fat under the hub;
  - centre: the web, then the Emperor.
- **Revisits change:**
  - A snuffed lantern's hawkers move to the next lit place, so the rooms still to come get busier.
  - Beetles come up to eat under the dead lanterns.
  - Every room on the west loop shows a claws lock the first time through.
  - The loops re-enter rooms from new sides, so a revisit is a new route through the room, not a walk back.

## The secrets layer
Every secret has an honest tell in the world:
- **The moth-stone**, behind the one leaf curtain that doesn't sway. Without it there is no map.
- **The Wing Midden:** the hub pool's "rock" breathes. Three pogos and the old burrback wakes: an optional
  mini-boss, worth a moon-drop.
- **The trunk's shadow**, opened when the web's skirt lifts.
- **The cold lantern:** a reflection with no source.
- **The Crow's Eye:** a "star" sitting too low. Long breath.
- **The Orbit:** an optional, genuinely hard fling ring round the outside of the web, embers on a miss, sharp wings
  at the end. It is the lens in one challenge: she does on purpose what the moths do helplessly. A skilled player
  can use it to reach the east before claws.

## The boss: the Emperor
I chose this over another spider on purpose. The Emperor is a great dusk dragonfly, the apex of the Sun's food chain,
and you have watched it cross the Sun as a silhouette in every room. It is easy, like the Weaver, with one lesson per
phase, each from a loop:
- **P1, the orbit:** it circles on a readable ellipse and dives with a silver flare. A fling-and-cut into its path
  knocks it out of orbit (the Crook's lesson).
- **P2, the swing:** it hits the chain and the Sun swings, and now it strikes only what the swinging light touches.
  She rides the moving shadow on the faces with claws, and it overshoots into the dark (the east loop's lesson, and
  the pillar "the dark is safe" as a fight).
- **P3, the fall:** wings torn, it clings to the swinging Sun. She pogoes on its back and on the cage flames, then
  cuts the last link (the Skein's lesson).

Her cocoon is the moon patch, 20 s away.

## What it borrows
| Game | What it borrows |
|---|---|
| **Dark Souls** | Firelink's "the elevator comes out at Firelink"; the two bells as two loops in either order |
| **Super Metroid** | Brinstar's shafts, which you re-enter from a door you couldn't reach |
| **Metroid Dread** | One-way drops into long loops that close with a shortcut back to a known room |
| **Hollow Knight** | Dirtmouth's well, and a hub sitting under the thing that matters |
| **Ori** | The Spirit Tree as a visible centre |
| **Animal Well** | Honest, wordless tells |

## Biggest risk
**The click depends on recognition across room fades.** We build rooms, not one seamless world, so "it comes out
THERE" can fail if the player doesn't recognise the place from its new side. The mitigations:
- each crossing lands on a unique prop she touched before: the bask spot, the bolt, the cocoon;
- the Sun's bearing must be drawn truly in every backdrop, which is new render work (a world-position parallax for one
  object) and has to be right everywhere, or the compass lies.

The second risk is that three lanterns read as "collect 3 keys". What answers it is the free order of the first two
and the fact that each lantern is a *place* on a different side of the Sun, not an item.
