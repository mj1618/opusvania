# 2. The Moonstair

**Lens:** one great vertical place. The whole area is a single giant mountain ash, about eleven screens tall: the
last firefly's first lamp-tree. ~24 minutes for a first-timer from the hatch to the hub's way out, ~35 with the
secrets.

## Through-line
She fell from the top of the tree, and she wants to get back up. The stragglers of her swarm are circling the false
lamp in its crown, and above that lamp is the moon her kind fly by. **The goal is in view straight up from almost
every screen:** the crown lamp's warm glow with the moon over it.

## The arc
**Fall → floor → climb → silence → flow → ascent → peak → dive.**

1. **The Lampfall.** Wing hatches on the crown's highest twig. She sees the tail of the swarm swerve into the crown
   lamp, chases the rest off the twig's end, and falls the whole height of the tree. The fall is steerable and lasts
   about 20 s. It previews the level from top to bottom.
2. **The climb up.** She lands in a drift of dead wings and climbs: the floor, the scar, the hub, the limb, the face.
3. **The Weaver** at the top.
4. **The dive.** She drops the whole tree again, this time as its master.

The same fall, twice: helpless at minute 0, chosen at minute 22. A first-timer could tell it back in one line: "I
fell out of the tree, climbed back up it, killed the spider at the top, then dived all the way down."

## The map logic
Height is progress, and every tier is a different place.

| Tier | Where | What it's like |
|---|---|---|
| Floor | the Wingfall | wings, beetles, glow-caps |
| Lower trunk | the Lightning Scar | inside pale split wood, danglers |
| Fork | the Hollow (hub) | dark, still, a pool of moonlight |
| Limb | the Hawker Limb | open air over a ten-screen drop, dragonflies |
| Upper trunk, outside | the Stringybark Face | bare bark, a bombardier, wind |
| Upper trunk, inside | the Silk Galleries | spider apartments, optional |
| Crown | the Crown Web | the web and the great lamp |

**One food chain explains the whole stack.** The lamps kill moths, and their wings rain down. Every predator feeds
at its own height:
- the Weaver at the biggest light;
- hawkers in the open air at the limbs;
- danglers on the inner faces;
- beetles on the floor;
- the old burrback under everything, fat on years of falling moths.

**The trunk's core is the spine.** The scar, the Hollow and the chimney are stacked in one column:
- The Hollow has a hole in its floor (the scar below) and a silk plug in its ceiling (the chimney above).
- The critical path goes up the **outside** (the limb, then the face) because the inside is choked.
- Killing the Weaver opens the inside. That opening is the area's shortcut, the dive, and the moon's way down.

**Falls reveal layers.** Before glide, a miss drops her one ledge, never a whole tier. After glide, a fall is a
route. The undersides of things (the limb's knothole, the chimney's inner alcoves, the scar's downward ledges) are
seen only while falling.

## Best moments
1. **The Lampfall (0:20).** A playable trailer. In about 20 s of falling she sees:
   - the Weaver's web glittering behind her;
   - a warm light moving behind a split in the bark (the loft);
   - moonlight through the hub's knothole.

   Then she lands in falling wings and looks up, and the whole climb is above her.
2. **The limb's tip (12:50).** After two tight vertical spaces comes open air. She glides out under hawker dives to
   a vista: the valley's lamps with the swarm caught in knots, and the mountain under the moon.
3. **The crown (21:00).** The great lamp goes out and the moon floods in. Every moth she freed in the tree lifts
   past it with the stragglers. Gust comes from moonlight on her own empty cocoon: the area ends where she began.
4. **The Dive (22:00).** She drops four screens down the opened core in the moon column, gusting out alcoves that
   face inward and that no climb ever showed. Then she makes a choice:
   - open her wings onto the hub's ledge;
   - or never open them, and punch through the cracked floor where she got her claws, into a den the moon has never
     reached. The breathing she heard at minute 5 wakes up.

## Density on every tier, every visit
Every tier holds the same four layers: a creature at its height, a flame to fling from or snuff, something to cut,
and something to find. The things to cut are fronds, puffballs, shelf fungus, stringybark ribbons, and **silk
bundles: moths the Weaver has wrapped, cut free one at a time**. Moths are rescued by the blade as well as by lamps.

Dead moths fall past her the whole climb, so the ambient picture tells the stakes. **The Hollow is the one empty
room**, and it is empty on purpose: the low between the scar and the limb.

The return visit is new because of the direction:
- The Dive sees the core from inside.
- Gust reaches wicks behind thorns seen during the climb.
- The opened chimney turns hub-to-crown into a one-minute climb.
- After the fight the loft's panel links the galleries to the cocoons.

## The secrets layer
Every tell is honest and none is signposted.
- **The Taproot Den.** The old burrback (optional mini-boss) and a moon-drop. Tell: the breathing, cracked floor of
  the claws glade.
- **The Firefly's Loft.** Its workshop: the flash-pattern ring and a half-built chandelier, plus a moon-drop. Reached
  by a hard, hidden fling chain in the galleries. Tells: the warm light in the fall, and one gold-dusted strand.
- **The limb's underside knothole.** Long breath, reached by a hard chain of swinging lanterns. Seen only by falling.
- **The moth-stone.** Behind a pale, hollow-ringing wall in the Hollow.
- **The scar window.**
- **The chimney's inner alcoves.**
- **The wrong-way stump** in the first ten seconds.

There are two optional hard parts (the loft chain and the lantern ladder), and two moon-drops for health.

## The boss: the Crown Weaver
A vertical fight that climbs, so height is progress inside the boss too.

| Phase | Name | Exam | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| **P1** | the ring | fling | She flings between five anchor wicks while the Weaver runs the strands to wherever she lands (it hunts by vibration) |
| **P2** | the climb | claws, reading drops | The floor web falls and she kicks up a two-screen chimney between limbs while the Weaver drops and swings on silk (the danglers taught this all the way up). Cutting its thread drops it stunned. A fall costs height, not health |
| **P3** | the lamp | pogo, glide | It guards the great lamp: lunge, then winded with its abdomen up. Only a pogo lands |

Then the lamp goes out flame by flame. It is the Weaver Matt liked, still a gentle first boss, now built on the
fling and on height.

## What it borrows
- **Ori:** the Hollow Grove and the Spirit Tree, one tree as a world; the Ginso Tree as a climb and then an escape
  through its own core.
- **Celeste:** the Forsaken City climbed upward; a fall costs one screen.
- **Hollow Knight:**
  - King's Pass's wrong-way-first cave;
  - a hub with five exits, of which three are open;
  - False Knight's floor breaking into a hidden level, here as the plunge.
- **Nine Sols:** a rest right before the boss.

## Biggest risk
**Vertical monotony, and the camera.**
- **Monotony.** Eleven screens of bark can read as one long wall. Each tier must look and sound unlike the others,
  and the outside routes (the limb and the face) must give the open air its due.
- **The camera.** A 16:9 view shows little height, so "the goal is always above" needs the camera to lean up and a
  sky layer that carries the crown lamp and the moon.
- **Room fades.** Both falls cross many rooms, and a fade would break them. The core column (crown to taproot) and
  the opening fall's east side should be single tall rooms, or exits must keep momentum without a fade.
- **Fallback.** If the steerable Lampfall fails a real attempt, it becomes a short tumble with three camera holds.
