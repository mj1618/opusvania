# Ori DE's first area, read from the game files (2026-10-30)

Matt's Windows depot of Ori and the Blind Forest: Definitive Edition (Steam app 387290, depot 387291; Unity 5.3.2f1,
303 scenes) replaced the mined, partly hand-placed Ori data with measurements. Internal reference only: no game data
is in the repo, only numbers and our own maps. Data: `area.json`; write-up and measurements: `README.md`; scripts:
`tools/reference/mined/ori/extract.py` (one file, a subcommand per step, usage at the top).

## Method

1. **Scenes.** `globalgamemanagers` → `BuildSettings` gives the scene list (scene N is file `levelN`). Each scene's
   `SceneRoot` points to a `SceneMetaData` asset with `SceneBoundaries` (rectangles in world units), so the streaming
   scenes tile the world exactly. The first area is the 17 gameplay scenes inside the Glades' box (`sunkenGlades*` but
   the backgrounds, `spiritTreeRefined`, `upperGladesBelowSpiritTree`, `westGladesOverworldForestA`) plus 5 dependent
   art scenes (`sunkenGladesBackground*`), which also hold gameplay objects (one secret container is there).
2. **Objects.** Every GameObject with its world matrix (parents composed), layer and active state. A scene's root is
   saved inactive (it is switched on when the scene streams in), so "active" ignores the root. MonoBehaviour fields come
   from type trees generated from `Managed/Assembly-CSharp.dll`; cross-file references are resolved to names (a
   placeholder's `Prefab` → `GO:largeExpOrb`).
3. **Terrain.** Ori's ground colliders are `MeshCollider`s generated by the art tool (`CageStructureToolCollider`):
   open-sided strips (quads from z −0.5 to 0.5) along the walkable edges only, cut at the scene bounds, with no
   back faces. Projection to the plane is dropping z (all gameplay colliders straddle z = 0). The strips do not close
   into loops (392 open ends), so the fill uses their winding: the triangle normals put rock on the right of the
   vertex order in 91% of the edges that close. Per scene: rasterise the edges and the scene rectangles as walls at 0.2
   u, seed rock just right of every edge and air just left, fill each walled region by its seeds (nearest seed where a
   region holds both), clip to the scene, trace polygons. Doors, invisible walls and solid `Polygon` colliders are
   added on top; trial-build and later-act walls (`trialWall`, `blockOffAct*Wall`) are left out. One-ways are
   `GoThroughPlatform` and physics logs on layer `platform`; hazards are layer `killCharacter` + `DamageDealer`;
   water is `WaterZone` (its bounds are the transform's position ± scale / 2, not a collider).
4. **Things and enemies.** Pickups are `CollectablePlaceholder.Prefab` (orbs, energy / life / ability cells),
   `KeystonePickup`, `MapStone`, `MapStonePickup`, `GetAbilityPedestal` (Wall Jump, Charge Flame) and the
   `GetAbilityAction` in Sein's sequence (Spirit Flame). Enemies are `*EnemyPlaceholder` with a `Settings` block
   (Health, DamageOnTouch, loot, ranges) that the placeholder copies onto the spawned enemy; the shooter rows are
   `ProjectileSpawner`s. Saves are `SavePedestal`s. Ability-gated groups sit under an `ActivateBasedOnCondition` with a
   `SeinAbilityCondition` (Ability 15 = Spirit Flame).
5. **Rooms and route.** Rooms are the scenes. Joins are open runs ≥ 1.5 u along shared scene edges, curated by hand
   (open sky is not an exit) and gated from the door objects at them. The critical route is a shortest path through
   the open terrain between 27 waypoints (pickups, gates, saves in the order of play).
6. **Hero and camera** from the prefabs in `resources.assets`: `CameraController.FieldOfView` 60,
   `CameraOffsetController.DefaultZoom` 20 (and each scene's `DefaultCameraZoom` 20), `seinCharacter` capsule, run and
   gravity settings, `SeinJump`, `SeinWallJump`, `SpiritFlame` damage assets. Damage to Ori: `SeinDamageReciever`
   (decompiled) turns damage into ceil(damage / 4) life cells on normal difficulty.

**Exact:** scene bounds, collider edges, every object's position, enemy HP / contact damage / respawn, hazard damage,
door requirements, pickups, saves, the camera and the hero's numbers. **Approximate:** the rock fill between edges
(±0.2 u; regions with open strips are decided by the nearest edge), the route (a shortest path, no physics), room shape
labels, which gate sits on which join (read off the map). **Mined:** the randomizer's location names and what each
pickup needs from later areas. **Estimated:** order of play and minutes.

## The 5 most useful findings for our first area

1. **Space: 84 screens, 63 view-widths of route, in rooms of 1.5-11.5 screens (median 3.7), for a hero 32 x 54 px whose
   jump is 140 px.** Ori's jump is 2.6 body heights against our 3.4, its view the same 1920 px. So the Glades' gaps
   and ledges are cut tight to a short jump, and the vertical travel is only 22% of the route (29,528 of 133,674 px of
   movement): long horizontal bands, short shafts, one 10-screen climb (the Spirit Caverns) at the end. Our first area
   at our jump can use ~30% taller steps for the same feel.
2. **Density: 0.57 enemies per screen (48: 41 killable + 7 turret plants), plus 54 hazard shapes and 26 orb plants.**
   That is 3x the walkthrough-based estimate. Only 3 of 17 rooms have no enemy, and they are the rests: the start glade,
   the Wall Jump hollow, the Spirit Tree. The densest stretch is the first fight (Sein's hollow, 3 Hoppers in 2
   screens = 1.5 per screen) and the Spirit Caverns (13 in 10 screens). With orb plants and pickups counted, no stretch
   of route is longer than 2.8 view-widths without something to hit, take or avoid.
3. **Enemies are weak one at a time; the threat is hazards and numbers.** Spirit Flame does 2: slugs (7 HP) die in 4
   hits, the first Hoppers (10 HP) in 5. A touch costs 1 of 3 life cells (damage 1-4) or 2 (damage 5). Difficulty climbs
   by HP and damage on the same kinds, not by new kinds: first Hoppers 10 HP / 1 dmg → hub 12 / 4 → optional rooms
   20-40 / 5. Hazards are harsher than enemies: lasers and the polluted deep water kill outright, thorns cost 1-2 cells.
   Turrets that cannot be killed (7 shooter plants) turn the big climb into a timing problem.
4. **Secrets: 12 of 34 pickups are hidden, a median 0.34 view-width (644 px) from the route.** The signature trick (5 of
   12) is a pocket behind foreground art that fades when Ori walks into it (`TransparentWallB`); the rest sit behind
   torch barriers (2), under polluted water (2), behind a breakable or stompable (2), above a ceiling (1). Keys are never
   secrets: all 8 keystones lie in critical-path rooms, none behind a hiding trick. Every dead end (6) pays.
5. **How the Glades teach.** The first 4 minutes have no weapon and almost no enemies (a scripted Rammer you jump, a
   Piranha you avoid). The moment Sein gives Spirit Flame, 7 enemies and the first 2 keystones switch on across the
   area you just crossed (`SeinAbilityCondition`), so the walk back is the practice ground, then 3 gentle Hoppers (1
   damage, no respawn) are the first fight. Hint zones sit exactly where a move is first needed (jump higher, drop
   through a platform, the spring, Soul Link). The new skill is used within seconds: an energy cell waits on the wall beside the
   Wall Jump tree, 1,370 px (0.7 view-width) of route after it. Gates show their price long before (the 4-energy door in the first
   screen), while the keystone gates open within a minute of their keys.
