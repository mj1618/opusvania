## 3. What I think the game is about
- **The idea:** I hatched as a moth under a real moon. The others left in a swarm toward the moon and I'm chasing them. Along the way are **lamps**: warm false lights that pull moths in and hold them, and seem to feed the beasts that guard them. Putting a lamp out frees the moths it holds (they follow me for a bit) and calms its guard. **Moon patches** are the true light: rest, save, heal. So: real light against false light.
- **What the hero wants:** to catch up with the swarm. I never learned her name in play (no text names her). "Wing" means nothing to me from the game itself.
- **The small moth:** it trailed me on the cocoon branch, then was always *ahead* of me, resting at the moon patches (P1, P3), and I couldn't interact with it. My guesses: a sibling who hatched with me, a guide who knows the way, or a straggler too weak to fly. I only know its name ("Tatter") from a designer label. It's the most intriguing thing in the build, and it does nothing yet.
- **What I didn't get:** what freed moths *do* for me (no counter, no change), and why some lamps hang where I can't reach them yet.

## 4. What I'd change first (top 5)

1. **Hazards and falls must warp you to the last safe ground, consistently** (the thorn branch, the hatching-branch falls, the chase gaps). Make decorative bark read as "not floor". *HK:* every spike hit warps you to the last safe ledge for one mask. *Celeste:* a hazard sends you to the screen start, seconds away. Here one thorn branch chains 4 hits, and a fall in minute one costs 30 s.
2. **Teach and give feedback on the old burrback.** A clang and spark on a blocked hit, a visible shell. When it hits a wall: a big crash, it flips, a vulnerable tell. No contact damage during the stun or rest. Frame the camera on the gate. Fix the lamp throw that clears the gate. *HK's False Knight:* every hit visibly lands, the weak point is obvious, and the arena shows both walls. *Ori:* bosses teach their one rule in the first 10 s with a safe repeat.
3. **Make lamp cutting legible.** Let a cut while caught put the lamp out, or show the cut range on the core. Teach "cut from below" in the first wicks room with a lamp you can only reach that way. Show freed moths somewhere: a counter, the swarm growing behind you, or Tatter reacting. *HK:* every hittable thing reacts to the nail the same way. Here the same input sometimes cuts and sometimes gets me caught, depending on an angle I can't see.
4. **Fix the hub's navigation traps.** Make an opened shortcut grate walkable, as HK's opened floors are. Move it out from under the up-shaft, and fix the climb-up that lands under the hub floor. Move the one-way well's mouth away from the shelf landing, or give it a lip. *HK and Ori hubs* are safe to cross. Here I fell into the boss pit twice by accident while crossing the hub.
5. **Make glide and the early pogo forgiving, and readable.** Glide should start on *any* jump press in the air after the grace window, and the prompt should say "press jump again in the air". Give the thorn branch more clearance under it. Loosen the seed-case gap: a bigger ring or a slower fall after the bounce, plus a near-miss sparkle. *Celeste's* first dash screen and *Ori's* first bash are impossible to fail after you've seen them once. This one took me 8 tries and 2 deaths.

Smaller things: the cocoon's "press any button" should accept jump and move. The glide prompt is clipped off the left edge
(`shots/0225-gained-glide.png`). The first-lantern pillar needs a "not yet" sign, or I came in the wrong way, and the
room should tell me which. The moth companion needs one reaction when I reach it. In the hub and the well the camera often
left me at the screen edge.

**Why I stopped at 7:21:** I wasn't hopelessly stuck. The open threads all needed long backtracks after deaths: the
boss (never killed), the first-lantern pillar, the high lamps in the first-wicks room and the burrow, and wherever the
small moth goes. Without the agent's patience, I think a real player would have put it down at the second thorn-branch
death, or at the second accidental fall into the boss pit.
