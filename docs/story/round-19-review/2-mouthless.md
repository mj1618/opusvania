# Review: Mouthless (round 19, cold read)

## 1. Anchor gate: PASS

The litmus works as written: "a little moth with no mouth, against the Mouth, a living thing that swallowed the whole world". The cold reader's first sentence came back almost word for word. The lab, with its glass dishes, the poster of "a hand lifting a small light out of a drawn human head", and the Pong screen, makes "a brain grown from us" a midpoint revelation the player finds in play, mythic on top and technical underneath. The Mouth is on screen and active at world scale in every act: roots creep and mouths open in the ground (Act I), the mountain breathes and chases her down its veins (Act II), and the Grove is overrun and the city kept (Act III).

Owner's ideas: **bio-AI takeover: central. Moth saving the world: central. Moths ignored, then underestimated: central** (it is rule 1 and the climax). **Sentience: central for the Mouth** ("love, or had it only learned how to make the static stop?"), **minor for the forest and animals** (Dun's refusal carries it; the forest is a thin character).

## 2. Craft verdict: GREENLIGHT WITH NOTES

No foundation problems. The spine, the antagonist and the hero's central mirror (the one who can only give against the one who can only take) are sound. The notes below are about clarity and making the moral weight land.

## 3. At the bar

- **The mirror is the premise.** A mouthless moth (true of eri adults) against a thing that "only sees mouths". The hero's flaw, the stealth rule, the climax and the theme are one idea.
- **A reversal that changes what you've done:** "It has not been eating the world. It has been feeding it." Then "The beads she burned stay dark." This is Nine Sols material.
- **Images a player retells.** "A ball bounces between two bats and nobody plays". The maker in her case with the jars of feed "unopened in her lap". The cold reader remembered the first of these.
- **Setup and payoff carried by play.** The prologue caterpillar that wraps itself, the Night 5 caterpillar Vesper shivers with, and then the Mouth "does what the caterpillar did". The final choice is "played, not picked", and the true ending takes away glide, jump and dash one at a time.
- **Dun.** Dun goes from rival to the first servant who refuses, and ends lying "across the lantern's door, facing out". He has no words and is all action.

## 4. Problems, ranked

1. **The complicity reversal doesn't land (POLISH, highest).** The cold reader asked, "Is that meant to land?" Burning walls kills sleepers, but nothing marks it at the time or afterwards. *Fix:* when the Mouth breathes on Night 4, the burned beads should be the only ones on the map that don't swell, shown on screen and held for a beat. Tallow's wall says *EATEN* because he believes the cases are corpses. Let the player read that wall again after the reveal, unchanged. This also answers "his plan kills her": he never knew.
2. **Rule 1 contradicts itself (POLISH).** "Passes her by" sits next to "every lampwing lamp swings to it". *Fix:* make it one sentence. Lampwings still chase light the way fireflies do, and the Mouth never learns what they chased. Then "after she feeds it, it sees her" stays clean.
3. **Logistics gaps in Act III (POLISH).** It isn't clear how Sorrel gets from the shaft into a case. *Fix:* show the roots that strip his scales closing him in, as she runs on. "Her blade cuts her free; only gifts win" doesn't say what the player does. *Fix:* the blade cuts holds, and only set-down glints hurt the Mouth. "Kept moths don't die" clashes with the aged sister. *Fix:* "Kept things age, but never end."
4. **Too much epilogue (POLISH).** Spin ends on eight images. *Fix:* keep the grey hand, Tallow at his sister's side, the two still moths and the vixen's cubs. Cut Dun and the map beads (a closing silk shot already covers them).
5. **The forest is too faint for its sacrifice (POLISH).** "Its oldest friend" confused the reader, and the river of gold arrives without much feeling behind it. *Fix:* give the heart one recurring action, such as nudging a glint toward Vesper at every lantern she lights, so that the Night 6 gold reads as the last time it does that.
6. **Echoes and name (POLISH).** Fed bodies in pods recall the Matrix, and a hungry abandoned creature recalls Shriek. The specifics (the jars, the handprint, Pong) carry it, but the pod image should be visibly root and seed, never tube and tank. The hero is now **Eri**: rename throughout. Delete "Editor's cuts" from anything a player-facing team reads.

## 5. Scores

Anchor 9, relationships 8, clarity 7, idea 9, world 8, mechanics-as-theme 9, antagonist 9, ending 8, originality 7, Grove fit 9, balance 8.

## 6. At the bar? No, narrowly

Biggest gap: the player's own complicity. Nine Sols lands because Yi did the killing. This outline has the same material, sleepers burned by the player before they knew, but it doesn't make the player feel it. Fix problem 1 and this sits next to the references as an equal.
