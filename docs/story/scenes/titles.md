# Game title candidates

Code name: Opusvania. Storyline working title: *Mouthless* (an echo of Ellison's *I Have No Mouth, and I Must Scream*).
Clash checks are quick web searches (2026-10-14), not a trademark search. **★ = top 3.**

| # | Title | Form | Why it fits | Clash found |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **★ The Moth and the Mouth** | fable pair | The whole spine in five words: the one who can't eat against the thing that only eats. Reads like an old fable, the way *Ori and the Blind Forest* does. Alliterative and easy to say. | None as a game. *The Moth* storytelling card deck is a different product. |
| 2 | **★ Mouthless** | single word | Vesper's nature, and the reason the Mouth never saw her, in one word. Quiet nod to Ellison. Late in the game it turns: the Mouth's own dish has never been fed either. | None found. Sits close to *Mouthwashing* (2024 horror) in sound and search results. |
| 3 | **★ The Kept World** | place | Names the central question (kept safe forever, or spent?) and the Mouth's wrong kind of love: every sleeper in a case, fed, still. Sounds gentle, reads darker the longer you play. | None found. Nearby: *Keeper* (Double Fine, 2025), *KEPT* (visual novel, 2026). |
| 4 | **Givenlight** | coinage | Glints are light the forest makes to give away. All of Vesper's powers are given to her, and the ending is light given until the Mouth lets go. Giving, not taking, in one word. | None found. |
| 5 | **Unfed** | single word | True of the moth, who can't eat, and of the starving brain that feeds the world. It hides the twist inside the title. Bleak and spare, in the way *Gris* is spare. | None on Steam. An itch.io cat game is called *Unfeeded*. |
| 6 | **Mothsilk** | coinage | Cocoons, cases, lanterns made of hardened cocoon, and the ending, where the mountain spins itself shut. Tactile and ownable. | None found. Risk: "silk" sits next to *Silksong*. |
| 7 | **Vesper and the Mouth** | name + foe | Keeps the hero's name in the title, in the Ori form. | **Clash.** *Vesper* (Cordens, 2021) is a well-reviewed atmospheric 2D platformer. *Vesper and the Umbral Passage* already uses this "Vesper and the ..." form. The hero's name is a problem for any title. |
| 8 | **Glint** | single image word | The game's currency, and its smallest gift. A precious light in the dark. | **Crowded.** Several itch.io games are called *Glint*. *Glintseeker Island* is due in 2027. |
| 9 | **Shiver** | single image word | The last gift: Vesper spends her warmth, and the caterpillar learns to let go. Cold, small, bodily. | **Taken.** Two Steam games are called *Shiver*, both horror. |
| 10 | **Mothlight** | coinage | Moths drawn to light. The rule that it sees only mouths and light. | **Taken.** A 2017 Steam RPG and several itch.io games use it, as does Brakhage's 1963 film. |

## Recommendation
- **The Moth and the Mouth** is the strongest. It is mythic on the surface, says who fights whom, and is retellable. It
  keeps the Ellison echo without borrowing it, and the player's first sentence about the game is already in it.
- **Mouthless** is the most ownable single word. It works as a logo and a subtitle-free title. Keep it if the team
  wants something stark, like *Gris* or *Nine Sols*.
- **The Kept World** is the most thematic, and best if the team wants the title to carry the question, not the cast.
- **Avoid "Vesper" in the title.** An existing, well-reviewed 2D platformer already owns it. Consider whether the
  hero's name itself needs a second look.
