# Mouthless: cold-read retell (HK lore-hunter player)

## 1. The story in three sentences
A grown brain that people abandoned in a lab has covered the world in pale root and put everyone into sleeping cases, and it ignores moths because they don't eat. Umber, a moth with a week to live, warms that brain by accident, and after that it sees moths and comes for her wood, her brother and every moth there is. At its heart she has to choose between being kept forever, burning it and killing most of the sleepers, or giving her life with every moth there is so it can cocoon itself and let the world go.

## 2. Characters
- **Umber**: a cold-born moth; wants to save her wood, then everyone.
- **Sorrel**: her brother; wants to dance with his silver-edged partner, then to spend his time with Umber.
- **The Hush**: the dish-brain; wants to keep everyone fed and close, because every leaving hurts it.
- **Dun**: its armoured badger; wants to bring it things to keep, and later protects Umber.
- **Tallow**: the last man awake; wants to burn the cases and find his sister.
- **Tallow's sister**: she grew the brain from her own hand, came back to burn it and chose to be kept.
- **The forest**: an old mind; wants to give its warmth away.

## 3. The enemy
The Hush is lab-grown neurons that people trained with a ball game and punished with static when they missed. It was wired up to feed the town. When the lab closed and everyone walked out, each leaving felt like static, so it took people into cases to stop the hurt. Now nothing wakes and nothing is born. It wants no one to leave ever again.

## 4. Endings
- **Spin**: moths warm it until it cocoons, people wake, Umber and Sorrel die, the forest is spent, and something presses back against the sister's hand.
- **Stay**: everyone is warm and kept forever, and nothing is born again.
- **Burn**: the Hush dies and takes most sleepers with it, Tallow's sister among them, and Umber has one last dance in a dead wood.

## 5. Confusions
- On Night 4 I had to re-read "if she kept the coal". Setting the coal in the lantern seems to use it up, and burning the wall keeps it as the "red coal". The choice is clear in hindsight, but I couldn't tell at the time that it locks the Burn ending.
- In Burn, Sorrel's partner dances at the summit, but she was sealed in a seed in the pool. Nothing says the seed opened.
- On Night 7 Tallow is sealed beside his sister, but it never says whether he chose it or was taken. The unlit torch suggests he chose it.
- The dance happens at the dead oak, but Burn says "the summit for the first time". Are they the same place?
- Where does Sorrel get the glints he "blazes" with on Night 6?
- Why was the town hungry when the first root went down the pipe? Because the lab closed?

## 6. What stays with me
The sister's memory. She walks back in with a torch, pinches it out and puts her hand on the glass. Then, in the last image, something presses back. **Yes, I'd buy and finish it.** The poster, the coat and the dishes of "brothers and sisters" are exactly the trail of item text I chase. I'd also replay it to see what the coal costs.
