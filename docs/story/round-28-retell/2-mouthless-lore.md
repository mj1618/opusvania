# Mouthless: cold retell (player 2, lore hunter)

## 1. The story in three sentences
A pale living growth called the Hush has put the whole human world to sleep in hanging cases, feeding everyone and keeping them close, and it never notices moths because moths don't eat. Umber, a moth with a week to live, travels from her wood to the mountain at its heart and finds it is a brain grown in a lab dish from a scientist's cells, abandoned when the lab closed, so it took the world so no one could ever leave it again. When she warms it, it finally sees moths and wants to keep them too, and in the end she can be kept, burn it (killing most sleepers), or spend her life with every moth warming it until it spins itself into a cocoon and lets the world go.

## 2. Characters
- **Umber**: save her wood, then the world.
- **Sorrel**, her brother: dance with his partner, and more time with Umber.
- **The Hush**: feed and keep everyone, because each leaving hurts.
- **Dun**, a root-grown badger: bring the Hush what it wants kept; later turns and guards Umber.
- **Tallow**, last man awake: burn the cases and find his sister.
- **The forest**: give its warmth away.

## 3. The enemy
A lab-grown cluster of cells that learned a ball game under punishing static. Abandoned, it sent root into a hungry town and found that keeping people stops the static. Forty years on, the world sleeps in cases, the first city is a breathing mountain, animals are regrown as servants. It wants no one to leave.

## 4. Endings
- **Spin**: all the moths warm it until it cocoons itself into a sleeping moth-shape; people wake; Umber and Sorrel die; the forest is spent.
- **Stay**: Umber is kept warm forever; nothing is ever born again.
- **Burn**: the Hush dies, and nearly every sleeper with it, including Tallow's sister.

## 5. Confusions
- Midpoint: keeping the coal means "burning through" the wall. With what? If the coal, why do I still have it for Burn?
- Spin says "every moth the Hush never noticed", but after the turn it noticed every moth.
- Spin costs "glide, then jump, then dash": dash and glide were never introduced as abilities.
- Sorrel's partner, kept for days, then flies home on "her last night". Does a case pause a moth's week? Stated nowhere.
- The caterpillar scene on Night 5: I had to think to link it to the cocoon ending.
- Was the Hush grown from the sister's cells? The poster implies it; I wanted it confirmed in an item.

## 6. What sticks
The sister laying her palm on the silk, and something pressing back. Yes, I'd buy and finish it: the mouthless/eating idea pays off in the ending, and I'd hunt every item text.
