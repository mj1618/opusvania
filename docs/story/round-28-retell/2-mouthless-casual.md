# Mouthless: casual player retell

## 1. The story in three sentences
You're Umber, a tiny moth with a week to live, in a world where a pale fungus-like thing called the Hush has put every person and animal to sleep in pods, and it ignores moths because they don't eat. You fight your way to its heart and find it's a lab-grown brain that was abandoned and has been "feeding" everyone ever since so nobody else leaves it, and when you warm it, it suddenly notices moths and comes after your kind. At the end you choose: get kept in a pod forever, burn it (killing most sleepers), or give your whole life to warm it until it lets go.

## 2. Characters
- **Umber**: the moth; wants to save her wood, then everyone.
- **Sorrel**: her brother; wants to dance with his partner, then spend time with her.
- **The Hush**: the brain; wants everyone fed and close because people leaving hurts it.
- **Dun**: a badger overgrown with root, its servant; brings it things to keep, then switches to guarding Umber.
- **Tallow**: last awake human; burns pods, looking for his sister.
- **The forest**: an old slow mind that gives its warmth away.

## 3. The enemy
A brain grown in a dish, left alone when the lab shut. It spread root over the world and sealed everyone in pods, so no one has woken or been born in forty years. It wants to keep everyone, because every leaving hurts it (the "static").

## 4. Endings
- **Spin**: Umber and every moth warm it; it cocoons itself, people wake, Umber and Sorrel die.
- **Stay**: Umber sleeps in a pod; everyone kept warm, nothing ever born again.
- **Burn**: the Hush dies and takes most sleepers, including Tallow's sister, with it.

## 5. What confused me
- Was the brain grown *from* Tallow's sister? The poster shows a woman in a greenhouse coat, and his sister wears one. I couldn't tell if she was its "mother" or just a worker.
- Timeline: forty years asleep, the sister gone twenty, the lab closing, "twenty years later". I had to stop and do the maths.
- The coal choice at the wall on Night 4 quietly decides whether the Burn ending exists. I'd miss that.
- Sorrel's partner's pod is seed-sized, gets dragged into the pool, then lifted out at the end. Hard to track.
- Wingtip taps happen so often I lost what they mean.
- Why does Spin work when closing all the pits didn't? "Too cold to spin" is the answer, but it's subtle.

## 6. What stuck, and would I buy it
Umber folding her wings over the badger that crashed through the ice and shivering until its static stops, then later Dun snapping his own root and lying across her lantern door. **Yes**, probably. A dying moth giving warmth instead of fighting is a hook I haven't seen, and the badger turning is a payoff. But the lab lore is heavy, and I'd need the game to show it, not tell it.
