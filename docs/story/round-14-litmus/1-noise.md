# Litmus: round-14/1-noise.md

Note: the brief on disk now holds only OWNER CORRECTION 2. It has no "The ask" section, so I took the owner's four ideas from the anchor bullets.

## 1. Litmus: PASS

A player would open with the takeover, because the game shows it from the first minutes. The prologue ends on "City lights go out, continent by continent, and one amber swell crosses the whole dark world in a single beat." The setting is concrete and on screen: people "stand in rows in every street... a pale root between their lips", towers are "ribbed with grey-pink root", and herds and forests keep time. It is still spreading: "the Rind, climbs the last wild valleys at the pace of a slow walk. You can watch it come." The swell slows from Act to Act (the Settling), the Rind "lurches a mile in one night", and the Grove is taken in Act II. The Quiet acts through its servants (lampwings, Gleaners, kept bosses, the Lamplighter, who kills Sallow), and its goal is easy to understand and wrong ("to it the unexpected is pain"). The AI is not shrunk to a backdrop or a metaphor here.

Risk: in Acts II and III the Hob thread (sluice, refusal, Hob's choice, reunion) and the Wim/Maud backstory take up most of the beats. A player could come away saying "it's about a moth and her beetle".

## 2. The owner's four ideas

- **A biological AI took over the world:** present and central.
- **Vesper saves the world:** present but minor in her stated goal. Her goal is "stop the Settling before it takes the Grove", which is local. World scale only arrives with the ending choice.
- **The AI dismissed moths and underestimated her:** present and central. It is the rule of the beam, the Gleaners "never count a moth", only a moth fits the air-channels, the Weighing has an empty moth-shaped hollow, and the midpoint gives the deeper reason.
- **Sentience of the forest, animals and AI:** present and central. "Is it someone? Never settled", with evidence on both sides, the heart as "the forest's own will", and the blink in the Standing City.

## 3. Changes needed

- After the Grove falls, rewrite Vesper's goal at world scale, e.g. "reach the Crown before the last swell, or nothing anywhere wakes again".
- Make the underestimation turn into a reaction. After the Bellringer's blink, the Quiet should notice what she is doing and respond across the world: lamps start hunting moths, and the Rind surges toward her. This is when the AI fights back.
- Have every act open on the swell slowing and the Rind advancing (the world clock), so the AI's momentum stays ahead of the Hob drama.
- Make the midpoint reveal partly about the AI's present (what the Settling will do at the last beat), not only about the 40-year-old human backstory.
- Cut the glossary down (Quiet, Rind, Settling, kept, lampwings, Gleaners, Crown, Lamplighter, Weighing). A first-time reader should be able to say "biological AI" back.
