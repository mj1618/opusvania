# Litmus check: 5. ONE NIGHT (round 14)

Note: the scratchpad brief only contains the OWNER CORRECTION 2 section. There was no "The ask" section to read, so the owner's four ideas are taken from the correction and the relayed owner quote.

## 1. LITMUS: PASS (narrowly)
The AI is world-scale, on screen and moving. "Every person on Earth is held." Cities are "wrapped in it like coral... The world has a heartbeat, and it is the Calm's." It has momentum: "It is spreading tonight... half the Grove held by the coldest hour." Its servants come to you ("Every kindling brings a fight to you"), and three of the four bosses are ridden by it. The Mast pull-back says it plainly: "Not a valley. Everything." It is not shrunk into a backdrop or a metaphor.
Risk: the Calm's will only acts through riders and "the look". Vesper wins by standing still inside a picture that generations of moths drew. A player might lead with "a moth lighting lamps to draw a picture in one night" before saying "an AI that took over the world." The story is about the Calm, but the verbs belong to the moths.

## 2. The owner's four ideas
- **Biological AI took over the world**: present and central. It is grown from brain cells in a dish ("a steady beat felt good"), it took the world in the Calm Hour, and its goal is understandable ("That nothing is ever lost again").
- **Vesper saves the world**: present and central. In the Seen ending "it lets go of the world" and the cost is real: Vesper, and "No moth ever dances again." In the Unseen ending the world is lost.
- **AI dismissed moths, underestimated her**: present and central. "Are the moths a danger? ... no." It shows up in play: moths fly through seals, riders hunt lamps rather than moths, and the climax is "For the first time it sees one."
- **Sentience of forest, animals and AI**: the AI part is central ("Is it a someone? Both, never settled", the Querystones, roots carving the answer). The animals and forest are present but minor: the Matriarch is "forty years tired" and lies down in the light, but nobody asks whether the forest is a someone.

## 3. Changes needed
- Give the Calm one direct, felt act against Vesper partway through, such as the Eye glimpsed early, or the front visibly racing her on the dawn run, so the antagonist presses on her and isn't only a field of effects.
- Give Vesper an active final verb before the stand-still, for example cutting the Eye's last ridden guardian or kindling inside its tissue, so that "taking on" fits.
- Make the sentience of the forest and animals matter once: a held stag or the freed Matriarch choosing something that the Calm's loops would not.
- Cut one mechanism (coals or hoods-as-decoys) for clarity.
