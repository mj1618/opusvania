# Mouthless: a retelling by a first-time reader (Ori fan)

## 1. The story in three sentences
A moth called Umber, with one week to live, sets out from her wood to stop the Hush, a pale growth that has put the whole world to sleep in hanging cases. She finds out it is a brain people grew in a dish and then walked out on, and that it has been "feeding" everyone ever since so nobody can leave it again. When she warms it, it finally sees moths and wants to keep them too, so she has to choose between staying with it, burning it, or spending the rest of her life to wrap it in a cocoon so it lets the world go.

## 2. Characters
- **Umber**: a small, cold-born moth; wants to save her wood, and later the world.
- **Sorrel**: her brother; wants to dance with his partner, then more time with Umber.
- **The Hush**: the grown brain; wants everyone fed and close, because every leaving hurts it.
- **Dun**: a badger turned root-armoured servant; at first brings the Hush things to keep, then guards Umber.
- **Tallow**: the last man awake; wants to burn the cases and find his sister.
- **The forest**: an old, slow mind; wants to give its warmth away.

## 3. The enemy
The Hush covered the human world in cool tissue, pulled people and animals into cases fed by stalks, stopped all births, and turned a city into a breathing mountain. It wants nobody to leave it, so the static (its pain) stops.

## 4. Endings
- **Spin:** Umber and Sorrel give their lives circling the dish; it becomes a cocoon, the world wakes, and the forest has nothing left to give.
- **Stay:** Umber lies down in a case; everyone is warm and kept forever, and nothing is ever born again.
- **Burn:** she drops Tallow's coal in; the Hush dies and takes most of its sleepers with it, and Umber gets one dawn dance.

## 5. What confused me
- Sleepers age or not? The partner wakes "no older," but people wake "old," and the sister is "old now."
- In Burn, "only those kept days, not years, wake," yet the wood ends with "nothing else moves." Weren't the moths cased on Nights 5-6 only days ago?
- How does a brain in a dish feed greenhouses and dinner tables? The poster says so, but I never pictured it.
- Tallow survived twenty winters awake. How? Also he lies down in a case between Night 6 and 7 off-screen. That's a big turn to skip.

## 6. What I'd remember
Sorrel folding his wing over newborn Umber, shivering, tapping her wingtip, and then the same gesture at the very end when she can't walk. Yes, I'd buy it and finish it. The Hush is a monster I'd grieve for, and Sorrel is the heart. Umber herself wants something a bit generic, though.
