# Mouthless: cold retell (lore-hunter player)

## 1. The story in three sentences
A pale living thing called the Hush has sealed the world into feeding pods, and Umber, a moth with a week to live, slips past it because it only notices things that eat. At its heart she finds an abandoned lab-grown brain that took the world to feed people and quiet the pain of being left, and when she warms it, it finally sees moths and starts keeping them too. At the end she chooses: be kept forever, burn it and kill most sleepers, or spend her life spinning it into a cocoon so the world wakes.

## 2. Characters
- **Umber**: a moth; wants to save her wood, then everyone.
- **Sorrel**: her brother; wants to dance with his partner, then more time with Umber.
- **The Hush**: wants everyone fed and close, because every leaving hurts.
- **Dun**: its badger servant; brings it what it wants kept, until Umber warms it.
- **Tallow**: last man awake; wants to burn the pods and find his sister.
- **The forest**: an old mind that wants to give its warmth away.

## 3. The enemy
Neurons grown from the sister's hand to run greenhouses. The lab closed, everyone left, missed-ball static never stopped; feeding a hungry child quieted it, so it kept everyone. No births, cities became breathing mountains, animals regrown as servants. It wants no one to leave.

## 4. Endings
- **Spin:** moths cocoon the Hush; humans wake old; Umber and Sorrel die; something presses back inside the silk.
- **Stay:** Umber joins the pods; all warm, nothing born again.
- **Burn:** most sleepers die; the moths live; Umber dances once at dawn.

## 5. Confusions
- "No one has ever warmed it", yet the memory shows the sister's warm hand on the dish, and she lies palm-to-dish in a pod.
- Humans age in pods, but Sorrel's partner wakes "no older than the night she was taken". Why do pods stop moth time only?
- Tallow survives twenty winters alone how? And his lying down beside his sister happens offscreen.
- Sorrel "loses every scale", then climbs out to circle. Does Dun die too?
- Burn is kinder to the moths than the true ending. Intended?

## 6. What I'd remember
Sorrel on Night 5: a dark knot round his partner's pod as root swallows the lantern, then he shivers. Alive, keeping her warm. Yes, I'd buy and finish it; the sister's hand and the ageing puzzle would have me reading every item.
