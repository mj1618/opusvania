# Mouthless: cold retell (lore-hunter player)

## 1. The story
A brain grown in a lab dish was left in the cold. To stop its pain it grew root over the world and sealed everyone in sleeping cases, but it never noticed moths, because moths don't eat. Umber, a moth with a week to live, gives the forest's warmth away to reach it and warms it, and it finally sees moths and hunts them. At the end she can be kept forever, burn it, or spend her life so it spins a cocoon and lets the world wake.

## 2. Characters
- **Umber**: save her wood, then the world.
- **Sorrel**, her brother: dance with his partner, then more time with her.
- **The Hush**: keep everyone where they can't leave.
- **Dun**, its old badger-servant: brings it things to keep, then guards her lanterns.
- **Tallow**, the last man awake: burns cases to spare people being "eaten"; wants his sister.
- **The forest**: give its warmth away.

## 3. The enemy
A dish of neurons that learned a ball game and ran the greenhouses. When the lab closed it got cold and frightened. It sealed every person and animal in a case: forty years, no births. It wants nothing ever to get away again.

## 4. Endings
- **Spin:** every moth warms it until it cocoons itself; people wake; Umber and Sorrel die.
- **Stay:** everyone is kept warm forever; nothing is born again.
- **Burn:** the Hush and most sleepers die; Tallow's sister dies in his arms.

## 5. Confusions
- Night 1: three lanterns get lit, but only the coal lights lanterns, and she has none yet.
- "The big glint... from the high island": what island?
- Night 4: "every case breathes, except the burned ones." I couldn't tell what caused it or what it means.
- The lampwing targets dark Sorrel *before* Umber flies off, but moths are supposed to become prey only once she leaves.
- It "feeds" the sleepers, yet in Spin they wake "old and hungry".
- Forty years sealed, sister "kept twenty": I had to do the maths.
- Does giving the coal away on Night 4 lock out Burn? It's implied, never said.
- What is the forest? Is it a grown mind too?

## 6. What I'd remember
Sorrel curled as a dark knot round his partner's seed-sized case while root closes over the lantern, until one wingtip moves. Also the Spin laps, which cost glide, then jump, then dash. **Would I buy and finish it? Yes.** The item texts reward reading, the dish flashback changes how the prologue reads, and an ending you pay for with your own moves is something I'd tell friends about. Night 4 is too dense, though.
