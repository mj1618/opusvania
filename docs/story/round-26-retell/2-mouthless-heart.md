# Mouthless: cold retell (heart reader)

## 1. The story in three sentences
Umber, a moth with a week to live, flies out to stop the Hush, a pale growth that sealed every human asleep in cases and only notices things that eat. At its heart she finds a lab brain grown in a dish, abandoned and cold. The moment she warms it, it finally sees moths and wants to keep them too. She must choose: be kept, burn it along with most of the sleepers, or spend her last warmth so it cocoons itself and lets the world go.

## 2. Characters
- **Umber**: a moth born too cold to fly. She wants to save her wood, then the world.
- **Sorrel**: her brother. He wants to dance with his partner, then to have more time with Umber.
- **The Hush**: a dish brain grown huge. It wants to keep everyone where they can't leave.
- **Dun**: a badger regrown as its servant. Umber saves it, and it guards her lanterns.
- **Tallow**: the last man awake. He burns the cases out of mercy, his sister's among them.
- **The forest**: a slow mind that wants to give its warmth away.

## 3. The enemy
It began as lab neurons that learned a ball game and were soothed by a scientist's hand. Then they were abandoned in the cold, full of static. Its root sealed every person and animal in cases, and for forty years no one has woken or been born. It wants nothing to get away.

## 4. Endings
- **Spin**: the moths warm it until it cocoons itself. People wake, and Umber and Sorrel die together.
- **Stay**: she lies down in a case with her brother, and nothing is ever born again.
- **Burn**: the Hush dies along with most of the sleepers. Tallow's sister dies in his arms, and Umber finally dances.

## 5. What confused me
- The takeover says everyone on earth except Tallow sleeps, yet his sister was clearly awake for years afterwards. She comes back with a torch, and the Burn ending says she was kept twenty years, not forty. Who else was awake?
- Does it feed people or eat them? The spine says it feeds the world, and Tallow says EATEN. I never learned which is true or why it seals people.
- The idea of pain as static was abstract.
- The Night 4 coal choice comes back in the endings. I had to look back to see what "if she kept the coal" meant.
- In Stay, is Sorrel's partner real, or just a picture on the case's skin? In Spin she turns out to be alive in the pool.
- The "big glint from the high island" and the "summit" are never set up.

## 6. What stays with me
The wingtip tap. He gives it at her birth, at dawn when he leaves, and at the very end: "Neither moth rises." **Yes, I'd buy it and finish it**, because the brother story is my Ori prologue. My worry is that the Hush's motive reads like a lab note rather than a feeling.
