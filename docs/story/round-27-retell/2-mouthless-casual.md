# Mouthless: casual player retell

## 1. The story
A living root-brain called the Hush has grown over the world and sealed every person and animal in feeding pods, but it ignores moths because moths don't eat. Umber, a moth with one week to live, carries the forest's warmth to its heart and finds a lab-grown brain everyone abandoned, grabbing the world because it can't stand being left. When she warms it, it finally sees moths and wants to keep them too, so she chooses: be kept, burn it, or spend her life warming it until it lets go.

## 2. Characters
- **Umber**: moth, a week to live; save her wood, then the world.
- **Sorrel**: her brother; dance with his partner, then more time with Umber.
- **The Hush**: feed everyone and keep them, because every leaving hurts.
- **Dun**: old root-badger; fetches things for the Hush, then guards Umber.
- **Tallow**: last awake human; burns pods he thinks eat people, wants his sister.
- **The forest**: old slow mind; gives its warmth away.

## 3. The enemy
A dish-grown brain taught a ball game, then abandoned. Misses and departures felt like static, so it rooted into the world and pod-sealed everyone; nobody has woken or been born for 40 years. It wants everyone close so the static stops.

## 4. Endings
- **Spin:** every moth warms it until it cocoons itself; people wake; Umber and Sorrel die; the forest is spent.
- **Stay:** Umber is kept, dancing forever; nothing is born again.
- **Burn:** the Hush dies with most sleepers, Tallow's sister in his arms; Umber gets one last dance.

- Night 6 "He flies after his sister": Umber, or the partner's case?

## 6. What sticks
Umber shivering over Dun's snout on the ice, then Dun knowing her, snapping its root and lying across her lantern door. **Yes, I'd buy and finish it:** "a moth with a week to live versus a thing that never noticed moths" hooks instantly, the wingtip tap gets me, and the true ending is properly sad. The lab/memory middle I'd need shown, not told.
