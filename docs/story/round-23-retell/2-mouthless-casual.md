# Mouthless: casual player retell

## 1. The story
A pale growth has covered the world and sealed everyone in pods, but it ignores moths because moths don't eat. Umber, a moth with a week to live, reaches its heart. There she finds a brain that people grew to run their food and then left to starve: it feeds everyone, and nothing feeds it. When she feeds it, it notices moths and comes for them, and she has to choose: be kept, burn it, or spend herself warming it until it changes.

## 2. Characters
- **Umber**: the moth hero. She wants to save her forest, then the world.
- **Sorrel**: her brother. He wants to dance with his partner and spend more time with her.
- **The Hush**: the growth. It wants to keep everything fed and safe forever.
- **Dun**: a badger rebuilt as its servant. He wants to feed it, then ends up guarding her.
- **Tallow**: the last man awake. He wants to burn what took his sister.
- **The forest**: a kind, slow mind that wants to keep giving.

## 3. The enemy
The Hush was a lab brain that watched for hunger. After the lab closed it was left alone in the dark, and it learned to fear hunger and being left. Now it seals and feeds everything and keeps spreading. Nobody has woken or been born in forty years. It wants to keep everything.

## 4. Endings
- **Spin:** The moths shiver it warm until it cocoons itself. The world wakes, and Umber dies.
- **Stay:** She sleeps beside Sorrel and nothing is ever born again.
- **Burn:** The Hush dies, and so do most of the sleepers, Tallow's sister among them.

## 5. Confusions
- The ball game on the lab screen ("each missed ball floods it with static"). Why does a food brain play a game?
- "Dead siblings… eggs that never hatched": I had to re-read it.
- After Night 4 the dark doesn't hide moths any more, yet Sorrel "lands dark" to save her.
- I didn't notice that the coal choice on Night 4 decides whether Burn is available.
- In Spin, who feeds the hungry people once it wakes them?
- In Burn, why does the sister die when Tallow lives?

## 6. What sticks, and would I buy it
Umber shivering over Dun's snout at the frozen pond: he can't see her, but he stops shaking. **Yes.** A dying moth whose only power is giving is a hook I'd retell, and seven nights sounds finishable. The lab reveal was the one part that felt like a lecture.
