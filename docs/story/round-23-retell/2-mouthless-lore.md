# Mouthless: retold by a Hollow Knight lore player

## 1. The story in three sentences
A pale living growth called the Hush has sealed every person and animal on earth into breathing grey cases, but it never notices moths because moths don't eat. Umber, a moth with one week to live, crosses the sleeping world to its heart and finds it is a starving brain grown in a lab dish, abandoned, that feeds everyone it holds and is fed by no one. When she gives it light it finally sees moths and tries to keep them too, and at the end she has to choose: stay kept with her brother, burn it and kill most of the world, or spend her life shivering warmth into it until it spins itself into a moth and lets everyone go.

## 2. Characters
- **Umber**: save her wood, then the world.
- **Sorrel**, her brother: dance with his partner, then get more time with Umber.
- **The Hush**: keep everything forever so nothing is ever hungry or left alone again.
- **Dun**, an old burrback (a badger it re-grew): to be the one who feeds the Hush; later, to guard Umber.
- **Tallow**, the last man awake: burn what took his sister.
- **The forest**: keep giving.

## 3. The enemy
A lab-grown brain built to run the city's food. It was left to starve in the dark, so now it is afraid of hunger and of being abandoned. It covered the world in tissue and sealed everyone so nobody would be hungry. It isn't eating them. It feeds them. What it wants is to keep everything, and after the midpoint it wants to keep the one thing that gives.

## 4. Endings
- **Spin**: the whole moth colony shivers with her; the Hush becomes a moth in a cocoon; people wake; Umber dies beside Sorrel and Dun.
- **Stay**: both siblings are sealed and the world stays quiet. Nothing is born again.
- **Burn**: the coal kills every root. Nearly everyone dies, Tallow's sister among them. The moths are free.

## 5. Confusions
- In the lab flashback, "she is led out" right after Umber touches the stalk. I first read "she" as Umber.
- Burning through the wall of cases on Night 4 kills people before I know it does. Was that a trap?
- It "only notices things that need feeding", yet one gift lets it see *every* moth, including Sorrel, who carries no light. I accepted that, but it changes the rules.
- Is the forest a mind like the Hush, or something else? Both make one "first picture" of a moth, which suggests a link that never pays off.
- In Spin, people wake "old and hungry", and the thing that fed the world is gone. Who feeds them now?
- Calling the dead siblings "eggs" when they're dishes confused me.

## 6. What sticks
Dun crashing through the ice and Umber shivering over a snout that cannot even see her. Then, three nights later, Dun breaks its own leash to lie across her lantern. Would I buy and finish it? **Yes.** The world itself is the mystery, and every reveal changes something I already did, like the dots on the map. Seven nights is short enough that I'd want all three endings.
