# Mouthless: cold retell (Ori-fan reader, "heart")

## 1. The story in three sentences
A pale living thing has grown over the world and sealed every person in a grey case, and it never notices moths because moths don't eat. Umber, a moth with one week to live, fights her way into its heart and finds a brain that people grew to run the food, then abandoned. It has been feeding the whole world and starving itself. When she feeds it, it finally sees moths and wants to keep them too, so she has to choose: be kept with her brother, burn it, or spend herself warming it until it changes.

## 2. Characters
- **Umber**: a moth with a week to live; she wants to save her wood, then the world.
- **Sorrel**: her brother; he wants to dance with his partner, and later he wants time with Umber.
- **Dun**: the Hush's old badger-servant; he wants to be the one who feeds it, and he ends up guarding Umber.
- **Tallow**: the last man awake; he wants to burn the thing that took his sister.
- **The forest**: a slow mind under the Grove that wants to keep giving.

## 3. The enemy
The Hush is a brain grown in a dish that was left to starve. It grew over the world, sealed people and animals in cases, and turned badgers and fireflies into servants. It wants to keep everything so nothing is ever hungry or left alone again.

## 4. Endings
- **Spin**: every moth warms it, it becomes a sleeping moth, people wake, and Umber dies with Sorrel and Dun beside her.
- **Stay**: she lies down with her brother, and the world is kept forever with nothing born again.
- **Burn**: the coal kills it, a few people wake, Tallow's sister dies in his arms, and the world goes silent.

## 5. Confusion
- "Stay dark" says the Hush ignores moths, but its lampwings dive at any light. So does the Hush see light or not?
- There are two hearts, the forest's under a carved moth stone and the Hush's dish, and each one makes a moth picture ("the only picture", "the first picture"). Then Night 6 has dozens of pale moths on the walls. I mixed them up.
- After the midpoint, the dark "no longer hides" moths, yet Sorrel later "lands dark" as if that still protects him.
- The coal "never goes out" and makes her never dark, which seems to wreck the stealth rule.
- In Burn, why do only *a few* people wake?
- I only realised later that the burned map dots were living people, and that Tallow has been burning them for forty years. I had to re-read to be sure.

## 6. The moment I'd keep
Sorrel curled as a dark knot round his partner's seed-sized case while the root closes over the lantern.

**Would I buy and finish it? Yes.** The wing-tap between the siblings, Dun breaking his leash, and the shiver that comes back from the prologue to the caterpillar to the finale are Ori-level heart. The lab lore asks me to read a lot to understand the villain, but I'd cry at Night 5.
