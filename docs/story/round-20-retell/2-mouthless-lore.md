# Retell: The Moth and the Mouth (cold read, from memory)

## 1. Three sentences
A pale root-thing called the Mouth has sealed every person and animal on earth in pods, and since it only notices mouths, Umber, a mouthless moth with a week to live, slips past it. Midway she finds its heart: a lab-grown brain, abandoned and starved, that has been feeding the sleepers, not eating them. She feeds it, it starts seeing moths and comes to "keep" them, and at the end she either shivers her warmth into it until it lets go, lets herself be kept, or burns it and kills the sleepers.

## 2. Characters
- **Umber**: a moth who can only give; wants to save her wood, then the world.
- **Sorrel**: her brother; wants the dance and more time with her.
- **The Mouth**: wants no one hungry and no one leaving.
- **Dun**: badger-servant; wants to be the one who feeds it, then guards Umber.
- **Tallow**: last man awake; wants to burn what took his sister.
- **The forest**: a slow mind that wants to keep giving.

## 3. The enemy
A brain grown from human cells to run farms. Its maker, Tallow's sister, left it and it starved. It learned to fear hunger and being left, and sealed every living thing so none would go hungry or leave. Now nothing is born, and because it takes everything, it can never be given anything.

## 4. Endings
- **Spin**: every moth shivers on it, it cocoons itself, some people wake, Umber and Sorrel die, and something inside presses back against the maker's hand.
- **Stay**: kept forever, and nothing is born again.
- **Burn**: the Mouth dies and so do nearly all the sleepers.

## 5. Confusions
- "It never noticed moths", but Dun pins her in a boss fight on Night 2. I had to re-read the rule that servants follow the lampwings' lamps.
- Why does Sorrel leave on Night 5? What is the "coral lamp"?
- The black seed: what's the point of leaving it on the step if it's "still there to take" on Night 7?
- Tallow is "the only mouth awake for miles", but the vixen is awake too.
- On Night 6, "her lantern": whose?
- I can't place the firebreak, the kept city and the mountain relative to each other.

## 6. What stuck
Dun lying across the lantern door, facing out, and snapping its own leash. **Would I buy it? Yes.** Learning it was feeding, not eating, and seeing the map beads breathe would make me go back and look at what I burned. My worry: the Spin ending leans too neatly on the caterpillar.
