# Retell: 2-mouthless (the player who cried at Ori's prologue)

## 1. The story in three sentences
A living thing called the Mouth has sealed every creature on earth in root cases, but it can't see Umber, a mouthless moth with a week to live, because it only sees mouths. At its heart she finds a lab-grown brain, left to starve by its maker, that now feeds the whole world so no one is ever hungry or left again. Her gift makes it see moths and come for them, and at the end she chooses: be kept forever, burn it, or spend her life warming it until it lets go.

## 2. Characters
- **Umber**: mouthless moth who can only give; wants to save her wood, then the world.
- **Sorrel**: her show-off brother; wants the dance, then more time with her.
- **The Mouth**: wants no one hungry and no one leaving, ever.
- **Dun**: the Mouth's oldest badger-servant; wants to be the one who feeds it, then refuses it.
- **Tallow**: the last man awake; wants to burn what ate his sister.
- **The forest**: a slow mind that wants to keep giving, and is losing.

## 3. The enemy
A brain grown from human cells to run farms. Starved and abandoned, it sealed everyone in cases and feeds them through stalks: nothing born, nothing ends. It wants to keep everything.

## 4. Endings
- **Spin**: the moths warm it until it lets go and cocoons; most people wake; Umber and Sorrel die; spring comes.
- **Stay**: she's kept beside her brother; the world is fed and nothing is ever born again.
- **Burn**: the coal kills the Mouth and most of the sleepers with it; moths fly over a silent world.

## 5. What confused me
- The black seed on Night 4: leave it or keep it, but if you leave it it's "still there to take" on Night 7. So what did the choice do?
- Umber burns through a wall of living sleepers to get in, and nobody, her included, reacts.
- "Her flaw is that she hides": I never saw her hide or stop hiding.
- "Taking nothing, it has no warmth": why does feeding it make it cold?
- "A root rises behind Sorrel, stripped bare": is the root bare, or Sorrel, who just lost his scales?
- Tallow is "sealed in one of his own": one of his own what?
- The maker was "taken", yet climbs the mountain at the end; I had to work out she woke.
- Does the forest survive Spin? Its heart went dark on Night 6.

## 6. The moment I'd keep, and would I buy it
Dun lying across the lantern door, facing out, pulling until her leash snaps. **Yes.** I pity the Mouth, and Dun breaks my heart. But Umber and Sorrel need more small scenes together, or the two moths on the rim won't make me cry.
