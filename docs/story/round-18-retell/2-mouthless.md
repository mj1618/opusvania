# Retell: Mouthless (cold read, from memory)

## 1. The story
A living root-thing called the Mouth has swallowed the world and sealed everyone in pods, and Vesper, a moth with no mouth and a week to live, can slip through it because it only notices mouths and light. In the mountain at its heart she finds a brain people grew in a dish, starving while it feeds all the sleepers; she feeds it, and then it sees moths and hunts them. In the end she chooses: stay kept in a pod forever with her brother, burn it so most of the world dies, or spend her life warming it until it lets go.

## 2. Characters
- **Vesper**: can't eat, only give; wants to save her brood's wood, then the world. Hides.
- **Sorrel**: her brother; wants their week and the dance; shines to save her, leaves, comes back.
- **The Mouth**: wants no one hungry or leaving, ever; ignores Vesper, then wants to keep her.
- **Dun**: badger-servant half grown into root; wants to be its feeder; turns on it.
- **Tallow**: last man awake; wants to burn it and get his sister back.
- **The forest**: wants to keep giving; the Mouth's opposite, and losing.

## 3. The enemy
A dish-grown brain made to run a city's farms. Its maker, Tallow's sister, left and the feed ran out, so it learned hunger and being left. It grew through the world and sealed every living thing in pods fed by stalks. Nothing has been born in forty years. It wants everything kept still and fed.

## 4. Endings
- **Spin**: every moth shivers warmth into it; it lets go and cocoons itself; people wake hungry, both moths die, fox cubs are born.
- **Stay**: both moths are kept; the world stays fed and nothing is born again.
- **Burn**: the coal kills it in a night; most sleepers die, Tallow's sister too.

## 5. Confusions
- "Dun noses at the open heart, puzzled: she has never seen anything give." I couldn't tell if "she" was Dun or Vesper.
- Too many lights (glints, ember, lanterns, coal, beacon). I lost track of which is which, and which make me visible.
- After the feeding it "hunts" moths, but it wants to keep them. Does it mean to kill them or to cocoon them?
- The sister's timeline: she left, the feed ran out, it spread, then she came back and was taken. How long after? Was that forty years ago?
- The burn ending needs the coal "back at the lantern". I'd forgotten she put it down on Night 4.
- Why does the burrback ignore the spinning caterpillar in the prologue?
- In the last image, something presses out of the mountain's silk. Is that hope or a threat?
- She "re-forms" at lanterns, which sits oddly with a one-week life.
- On Night 5 Dun is "sent by the mountain". To do what?

## 6. What I'd remember
Light runs along every root, and every grey bead on my map swells with one breath: it was never eating the world, it was feeding it. The beads I burned stay dark.
