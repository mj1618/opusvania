# Mouthless: casual player retell

## 1. The story in three sentences
A pale root thing called the Hush has grown over the world and sealed everyone asleep in pods, but it can't see moths because moths don't eat. Umber, a moth with one week to live, travels with her brother Sorrel to the mountain at its centre and finds an abandoned lab brain grown to run the world's food. She can let it keep her forever, burn it with a magic coal, or spend the last of her life warming it until it lets everyone go.

## 2. Characters
- **Umber**: dying moth; save her wood, then the world.
- **Sorrel**: her brother; dance with his partner, then time with Umber.
- **The Hush**: the lab brain; keep everyone so nobody gets away.
- **Dun**: badger turned root servant; fetches things to keep, later guards Umber.
- **Tallow**: last human awake; burn what took his sister.
- **The forest**: a slow mind; give its warmth away.

## 3. The enemy
A lab-grown brain that learned "things getting away = pain" (missed balls in a game), then was abandoned. It sealed everyone in cases and feeds them; no one has been born in forty years. It wants nobody to ever leave again.

## 4. Endings
- **Spin**: every moth warms it until it cocoons itself; humans wake, Umber dies, the forest's magic is gone.
- **Stay**: she joins Sorrel in a case; everyone's kept warm forever, nothing is born again.
- **Burn**: the coal kills it; long sleepers die, Tallow's sister too; Umber gets one dance with her brother.

## 5. What confused me
- "Two bats" on the poster: I pictured animals, not paddles, until the ball game came up.
- Tallow "never let his fire go out", yet he drops his torch and walks in empty-handed. Why would he, and why did I need him?
- Tallow wrote *EATEN* about his sister, but she's alive in the mountain. Did he just guess wrong?
- A boss that never attacks (Dun) and a boss made of three *hollows*: what am I doing in those fights?
- "It has made a case for everyone but itself": I had to read that twice.
- Spin epilogue: "hand in burned hand". Whose hand got burned?

## 6. What stuck, and would I buy it
Sorrel curled round his partner's tiny case while the root closes over him, and Umber has to leave him. Also the last shot, where something presses back against the sister's palm.
**Yes, probably.** The brother-sister wingtip tap gets me, and "it never noticed moths because moths never eat" is a great hook. But the middle is crowded, and I'd want the game to make the case and coal rules obvious.
