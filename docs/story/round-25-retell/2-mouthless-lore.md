# Mouthless: a cold read (lore-hunter)

## 1. The story in three sentences
A living thing, the Hush, has sealed the world asleep in cases, and it ignores moths because they never eat. Umber, a moth with a week to live, follows its root to a mountain lab and finds a dish-grown brain built to feed the world, left in the cold, that sealed everyone in so nothing could get away. When she warms it, it sees moths and hunts them, and at the end she can join her brother in a case, burn it, or spend her last warmth so it lets go.

## 2. Characters
- **Umber**: wants to save her wood, then the world.
- **Sorrel**: her brother; wants to dance with his partner, then time with Umber.
- **The Hush (HEARTH)**: the dish-brain; wants everyone it feeds kept where they can't leave.
- **Dun**: an old root-grown badger; serves the Hush until Umber warms it.
- **Tallow**: last person awake; wants to burn what took his sister.
- **The forest**: a slow mind that gives its warmth away as glints.

## 3. The enemy
Dish-grown neurons that ran the world's food, taught by a ball game that every miss hurts. Abandoned cold, it sealed the world and stopped births for forty years. It wants nothing to get away.

## 4. Endings
- **Spin:** the moths shiver until it cocoons itself; people wake; both moths die; the forest is spent.
- **Stay:** Umber joins Sorrel in a case; everyone is warm forever and nothing is ever born.
- **Burn:** it dies, and so do the long sleepers (Tallow's sister too); the moths get one last dance.

## 5. What tripped me
- The sister "comes back with a torch" and is still sealed, yet fire is what keeps Tallow safe. Did she put it down?
- "Shut it down": then how is it running?
- How does the Hush know the forest's carved moth well enough to grow it on walls? A link, or a hole?
- Umber "fades on the road" after Night 4 with no cause given.
- Night 5: I thought the root had killed Sorrel until Night 6.
- "Hand in burned hand": why are the sister's hands burned?
- In Spin both moths die; Burn felt warmer for them. Intended?

## 6. What I'll remember
Dun snapping its leash and lying across the lantern door facing out. And the wingtip tap, from the prologue to the last scene. **Would I buy it? Yes.** The item texts are what I chase, the dish reveal pays off the prologue, and Spin's last image (something pressing back through the silk) would keep me theorising for weeks.
