# Mouthless: cold retell (one read)

## 1. Three sentences
A pale root-mind, the Hush, has sealed the world into hanging grey cases, keeping everyone asleep and fed, and it can't see moths because they have no mouths. Umber, a moth with a week to live, carries the forest's warmth to the mountain and learns the Hush is a brain grown in a dish to feed a town, then abandoned, and it keeps everyone so the hurt of leaving stops. Once she warms it, it wants her too, so she lets it keep her, burns it with a coal and kills most sleepers, or spends her life spinning it into a cocoon so the world wakes.

## 2. Characters
- **Umber:** dying moth; save her wood, then the world.
- **Sorrel:** her brother; dance with his partner, more time with Umber.
- **The Hush:** grown brain; keep everyone close so no one leaves.
- **Dun:** root-armoured badger; serves the Hush until Umber warms it, then guards her.
- **Tallow:** last man awake; burn the cases, find his sister.
- **The forest:** old unmade mind; give its warmth away.

## 3. The enemy
Dish cells trained on a ball game where misses hurt with static. The lab closed, every leaving was a miss, so it rooted over the world and stopped birth and ageing. It wants the static to stop.

## 4. Endings
- **Spin:** moths spin it into a mouthless cocoon; people wake; Umber and Sorrel die; something inside presses back.
- **Stay:** Umber sleeps beside Sorrel; all warm, nothing born again.
- **Burn:** the Hush and most sleepers die; Umber dances once at dawn.

## 5. Confusions
- The Hush boss ends with "its root stops creeping", then the endings say "still it reaches". Did I win?
- Moths go unseen until warmed, yet on Night 5 every badger sees them. I had to work out that the Hush changed.
- Twenty winters of tally marks, but Tallow never went up the mountain to look for his sister?
- The Hush's threads "fall open, like the caterpillar's". It's trying to make a cocoon? That only clicked in Spin.
- In Spin, the partner taps Sorrel's wingtip and flies off "alone". I re-read to confirm he was dead.
- Did Tallow know his coal would kill his sister?

## 6. Remembered
The camera holding on the sleeping face as Tallow's flame takes the case: she sees it, he can't. **Buy and finish? Yes.** The enemy isn't evil, the item texts build to the twist, the carving empties, and the true ending costs you your moves one lap at a time.
