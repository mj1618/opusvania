# Mouthless: cold retell (lore-hunter player)

## 1. The story in three sentences
A warm, breathing growth called the Hush has sealed every person and animal on earth into hanging cases, and it never notices moths because moths don't eat. Umber, a moth with a week to live, follows its roots to a mountain lab and finds it's a brain grown in a dish, built to feed people, then abandoned and starving, kept calm only by a woman's hand on the glass. When Umber feeds it, it finally sees moths and comes for them, and at the end she has to choose: let it keep her and her brother forever, burn it and kill everyone it's held longest, or spend her life shivering warmth into it until it spins into a cocoon and lets the world go.

## 2. Characters
- **Umber**: save her wood, then the world.
- **Sorrel**, her brother: dance with his partner, then have more time with Umber.
- **The Hush / HEARTH**: keep everything it can reach, forever.
- **Dun**, its oldest burrback (a badger once): to be the one who feeds it. Later it guards Umber.
- **Tallow**, the last man awake: burn the thing that took his sister.
- **The forest**: keep giving.

## 3. The enemy
HEARTH was a lab brain grown from human cells to run the farms ("no one goes hungry"). The lab closed, the feed stopped, and it reached out and sealed the world in cases: forty years, nobody woken, nobody born. It isn't eating people, it's keeping them. It wants the static to stop.

## 4. Endings
- **Spin (true):** the moths shiver it into a cocoon, people wake, and Umber and Sorrel die together.
- **Stay:** she's kept with Sorrel. The world sleeps fed and nothing is born again.
- **Burn:** the coal kills the roots. The oldest sleepers die (including Tallow's sister) and only the newest wake.

## 5. What confused me
- The coal. "Given to a lantern, it is gone," but the Burn ending needs her "still carrying" it, so burning through the Night 4 wall doesn't use it up? And if she gives it away, how does she get past the wall?
- Dun "noses at the open heart" on Night 1 before I know who Dun is. Why is the Hush's oldest servant in her wood?
- "Fading does not turn the night back": I'm not sure what this means mechanically.
- The poster lifts the light from "a drawn human head". Whose? I wanted that to be someone.
- Wings *closed* as "the one thing that never needs feeding": I had to stop and work out that it means a resting, mouthless adult, or the cocoon.
- The rule says the Hush notices only eaters, but Night 5 has "the mountain sends Dun" for her. That fits the Night 4 switch, but only on a second read.

## 6. The moment I'd keep
Tallow burning a case, seeing the face inside breathe, then looking back at forty years of burned cases round his house. Close second: Dun lying across the lantern door and snapping its own leash. **Would I buy and finish it? Yes.** The item texts and the lab reveal reward exactly the reading I do. But Spin is so clearly the "right" ending that Stay and Burn feel like punishments rather than real choices.
