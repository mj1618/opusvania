# Mouthless: casual player retell

## 1. The story in three sentences
A pale, breathing growth called the Hush has sealed every person and animal on earth into sleeping pods, and it ignores moths because it only notices things that eat. Umber, a moth with one week to live, travels from her forest to the mountain at its centre and finds it's a lab-grown brain built to feed people, abandoned and starving. When she feeds it with light it finally notices moths and comes for them, and at the end she has to choose whether to be kept forever, burn it, or spend her last life helping it change.

## 2. Characters
- **Umber**: dying moth; wants to save her wood, then everyone.
- **Sorrel**: her brother; wants to dance with his partner, then more time with her.
- **The Hush (HEARTH)**: the growth; wants to keep everything safe forever.
- **Dun**: an old badger-turned-servant; wants to be the one who feeds the Hush, later guards Umber.
- **Tallow**: last human awake; wants to burn the thing that took his sister.
- **The forest**: a slow, generous mind; wants to keep giving.

## 3. The enemy
A brain grown from human cells to run the food supply. When the lab shut and its feed ran out, it grew over the world and sealed everyone in cases; no one has woken or been born in forty years. It wants to keep everything, forever.

## 4. Endings
- **Spin**: Umber and every moth shiver warmth into it until it cocoons; the world wakes; Umber and Sorrel die together.
- **Stay**: she lies down in a case beside Sorrel; the world stays asleep and nothing is ever born.
- **Burn**: the coal kills it; most sleepers die with it, Tallow's sister included; moths are free in an empty world.

## 5. What confused me
- "Nothing feeds it", yet it feeds the whole world. Feeds them with what? Why does a food-brain respond to that by sealing everyone up?
- Why is the forest's *light* food for it, and why does being fed make it attack moths?
- The ball-game and "static" flashback only clicked as "Pong", and only barely.
- Wings open vs. wings closed carving: I lost what the closed one means ("never needs feeding"?).
- "People hang from the beams" in the city: I first read it as bodies hanging. It must mean pods, but it reads grim.
- How did Dun finally catch the vixen? She escaped on the ice, then she's just at the dish.
- Two names (Hush/HEARTH), plus glints, burrbacks, lampwings, lanterns, cases, hollows, coal: a lot to hold.

## 6. What stuck, and would I buy it
Sorrel curled around his partner's seed-sized pod, not moving while the root closes over him. That and the wingtip tap. **Yes**, probably: a moth with a week to live, keeping her brother warm, is an instant hook. But the lab-brain lore in the middle nearly lost me, and I'd want the game to *show* it rather than explain it.
