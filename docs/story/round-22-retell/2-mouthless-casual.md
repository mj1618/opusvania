# Retell: 2-mouthless (casual player)

## 1. The story in three sentences
A giant pale living thing called the Mouth has grown over the world and sealed every person and animal in grey pods, but it can't see moths, because moths have no mouths. Umber, a moth with one week to live, sneaks into its heart and finds out it's a lab-grown brain that was abandoned and starving, and that it was never eating people, it was keeping them fed. When she feeds it, it suddenly sees moths and comes for them too, and at the end she has to choose: get kept forever, burn it, or spend her whole life warming it so it can let go.

## 2. Characters
- **Umber**: a small moth with a week to live; wants to save her wood, then the world.
- **Sorrel**: her brother; wants to dance with his partner and have more time with Umber.
- **The Mouth**: the brain under the world; wants to keep everything safe and fed, forever.
- **Dun**: its badger servant; wants to feed it, later guards Umber.
- **Tallow**: the last man awake with a fire; wants to burn what took his sister.
- **The forest**: a slow kind mind; wants to keep giving.

## 3. The enemy
A brain grown from human cells, abandoned and starving. It sealed every living thing in fed pods so nobody goes hungry or gets left, because it fears both. It wants to keep everything.

## 4. Endings
- **Spin**: Umber and every moth shiver their warmth into it until it spins a cocoon; people wake, Umber dies beside Sorrel.
- **Stay**: she lets herself be kept beside her brother; the world stays fed and asleep and nothing is ever born again.
- **Burn**: she drops Tallow's coal in; it dies, and most of the sleepers die with it.

## 5. What confused me
- "Grown from us, to feed us": feed us *how*? A brain making food? I never got what its original job was.
- Tallow is "the last man awake," but the intro says "the few who kept a fire lit." Which is it?
- Why does a gift make it hunt moths? Had to re-read. The rules also spoil this ("From Night 4...").
- "Coral lamps" from the pool: never explained.
- Spin ending: what does the Mouth become? Is it hatching? Into what?
- Two beats before the choice (roots stop, static returns): had she already won?
- Dun's switch to Umber's side has no clear reason.

## 6. What I'd remember
Sorrel curled round his partner's tiny seed-sized pod while the root closes over the lantern, and the camera stays on him. Also brothers warming each other by shivering, which comes back at the end.

Buy and finish? **Yes, probably.** "A moth the monster can't see because it has no mouth" is a one-line hook, and the one-week timer gives urgency. The reveal lost me a bit; the siblings would carry me.
