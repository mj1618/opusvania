# Retell: The Moth and the Mouth (lore-hunter reader)

## 1. The story, to a friend
A pale living tissue called the Mouth has sealed every person and animal on earth in grey cases, and Umber, a moth who hatched with one week to live and no mouth, can slip past it because it only notices things with mouths. Halfway through she reaches its heart and finds it's a brain grown from human cells in a lab, abandoned to starve, and it was never eating the world: it was feeding everyone through stalks so no one would be hungry or left. Feeding it makes it see moths and come for them, and at the end she can stay sealed with her brother, burn it and kill every sleeper, or spend her last warmth with every moth so it spins itself into a cocoon and lets the world wake.

## 2. Who wants what
- **Umber**: save her wood, then the world.
- **Sorrel**, her brother: dance with his silver-edged partner, then more time with her.
- **The Mouth**: keep everything it can reach, forever.
- **Dun**, a badger-turned-servant half grown into root: to be the one who feeds it (then turns guardian).
- **Tallow**, the last man awake: burn what took his sister.
- **The forest**: keep giving.

## 3. The enemy
A lab-grown brain left in a dish to starve. It learned to fear hunger and abandonment, so it sealed everything and feeds it, while nothing feeds it. It wants to keep everything; what it actually needs is a hand on the glass.

## 4. Endings
- **Spin**: the moths shiver it warm, it cocoons, people wake, Umber dies beside Sorrel and Dun; something presses back against the sister's palm.
- **Stay**: both siblings sealed; the world kept, fed, never born again.
- **Burn**: the coal kills it and nearly every sleeper; moths fly over a silent world.

## 5. Confusions
- The title and the litmus give away the twist before I start.
- "Grown from us, to feed us." Was it a food AI? How does a brain feed anyone, and where does forty years of feed come from?
- Theme flips: the "truth" says it's starving, so the glints work, then the ending says it was never the feed, it was the hand. I had to re-read to see that this is meant to be a second reveal.
- Night 1 the root pulls off the Grove; Night 5 it pours from "the pool," which nobody mentioned before.
- Night 5 I thought Sorrel had died. Night 6 he flies off.
- The coal's text, "It wants everything": does "it" mean the coal or the Mouth? And if the coal keeps her lit, how does a Burn run sneak?
- Rule 1 says it sees moths from Night 4, which spoils the midpoint.
- Dun's turn on Night 5 isn't explained.

## 6. What stays with me
Every dot on the map breathes, except the line I burned. Yes, I'd buy and finish it: that dot detail and the Spin ending are exactly what I'd chase.
