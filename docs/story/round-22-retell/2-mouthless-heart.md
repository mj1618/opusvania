# Retell: "The Moth and the Mouth" (Ori fan, reading for the heart)

## 1. Three sentences
A pale living thing called the Mouth has sealed every person and animal in grey cases, but it never noticed the moths, because grown moths have no mouths. Umber, a moth with a week to live, reaches its heart and finds a brain people grew and abandoned: it was never eating the world, it was feeding it, out of terror of hunger. When she feeds it, it finally sees moths and comes for them too, and she must choose: be kept forever beside her brother, burn it, or spend her life keeping it warm until it spins itself shut and lets the world go.

## 2. Characters
- **Umber**, moth with one week: save her wood, then everything.
- **Sorrel**, her brother: dance with his partner, then more time with her.
- **The Mouth**, the grown brain: keep everything so nothing is hungry or left again.
- **Dun**, its oldest badger-servant: be the one who feeds it (later guards her).
- **Tallow**, awake forty years by his fire: burn what took his sister.
- **The forest**: keep giving.

## 3. The enemy
A lab brain left to starve. It grew over the world, sealed and fed every living thing, and made servants of badgers and fireflies. It wants to keep everything, and it is still starving because nothing feeds it.

## 4. Endings
- **Spin:** every moth shivers warmth into it; it spins shut, the world wakes, Umber dies under Sorrel's wing.
- **Stay:** she takes the case beside Sorrel; nothing is ever born again.
- **Burn:** the roots die and most sleepers die with them; moths fly over a silent world.

## 5. Confusions
- **Tallow's sister** is the hand that "turns off the lights and goes," and also the woman in the chair with *unopened* feed jars. Did she abandon it, or come back too late? This is the emotional hinge and I couldn't tell.
- "Tallow, the last man awake" against "the few who kept a fire lit."
- "Coral lamps" on Night 5: lampwings, or something new?
- Why Dun turns protector isn't motivated.
- Spin: what is born from the cocoon? "Something presses back" is lovely but vague.
- The Cortical Labs dev note pulled me out.

## 6. What I'd remember
A lampwing slowly turning its lamp onto Sorrel, who carries no light: my kindness is what made it see my brother. **Yes, I'd buy and finish it.** The wing-fold and wingtip-tap running from prologue to the last scene hit like Ori's opening, and "it was feeding the world" changes everything. The sister's thread just needs to be clear.
