# Retell: The Moth and the Mouth (cold read, lore-hunter)

## 1. Three sentences
A brain grown in a dish to feed people was abandoned and starved, so it covered the world and sealed every living thing in a feeding case so nothing would hunger or leave again. Umber, a mouthless moth it can't see, reaches its heart and feeds it, and that makes it see moths and come for her wood and her brother. She can be kept, burn it (killing the sleepers too), or spend her last strength warming it until, like a caterpillar, it lets go.

## 2. Characters
- **Umber**: dying moth, wants to save her wood, then the world.
- **Sorrel**: her brother, wants his dance partner, then time with her.
- **The Mouth**: wants no one hungry, no one leaving.
- **Dun**: its burrback, wants to feed it, ends up guarding her.
- **Tallow**: last man awake, wants to burn what took his sister.
- **The forest**: wants to keep giving.

## 3. The enemy
Neurons taught Pong, scaled up to run farms, then left. It grew tissue over the earth and turned animals into servants. It isn't eating the world, it's feeding it. Because it takes everything, nobody can give it anything.

## 4. Endings
- **Spin**: every moth shivers it warm; it frees the sleepers and cocoons itself; Umber dies beside Sorrel.
- **Stay**: kept with Sorrel; nothing is ever born again.
- **Burn**: the root dies and most sleepers with it.

## 5. Confusions
- It "never noticed moths", yet Umber is "*a* moth with no mouth", as if only she lacks one.
- Glints calm, but hurt in its mouths. Night 4's glints are a calming gift, Night 7's are damage. Fed or wounded?
- Why is the vixen the first animal ever to see her?
- "Every person on earth" is sealed, but Tallow is awake.
- "his sister's case, sealed in one of his own": I re-read it twice.
- The coal is never dark, and after Night 4 only dark hides her. Is Burn meant to be nearly impossible?
- Why do "some cases never open"? Is the thing pressing back at the end hope or threat?

## 6. What stays with me
Sorrel wrapped round the pale seed as root closes over the lantern, then that seed splitting on the pool in the epilogue. Would I buy and finish it? **Yes.** The Pong screen, the unopened jars and "feeding, not eating" are lore I'd dig for. Only the glint calm/hurt muddle bugs me.
