# Mouthless: casual player retell

## 1. The story in three sentences
A grey living growth called the Hush has swallowed the world, sealing people and animals in sleeping pods, but it can't see moths because moths don't eat. Umber, a moth with one week to live, carries the forest's warmth to the Hush's heart and finds it's a lab-grown brain the scientists abandoned, which took everyone so it wouldn't be left alone again. Once she warms it, it finally sees moths and wants to keep them too, so she has to choose between being kept forever, burning it, or spending her life so it can let go.

## 2. Characters
- **Umber**: little moth; wants to save her wood, then the world.
- **Sorrel**: her brother; wants to dance with his partner and have more time with Umber.
- **The Hush**: the grown brain; wants to feed everyone and keep them close because every leaving hurts.
- **Dun**: a badger in root armour serving the Hush; wants to bring it things to keep, then switches to guarding Umber.
- **Tallow**: last man awake; wants to burn the pods and find his sister.
- **The forest**: a slow old mind; wants to give its warmth away.

## 3. The enemy
A brain grown from a scientist's cells to run the food greenhouses. Abandoned when the lab closed, it rooted out and sealed everyone into fed, sleeping pods; nobody is born any more. It wants to keep everyone so the static of being left stops.

## 4. Endings
- **Spin**: Umber gives herself away, the Hush wraps itself in a cocoon, people wake up old, and Umber and Sorrel die together.
- **Stay**: Umber lets herself be kept; everyone is warm and asleep forever, and nothing is born again.
- **Burn**: she kills it with Tallow's coal; most sleepers die with it, and the three moths get one last dance in an empty wood.

## 5. What confused me
- "Press against the glass": which glass, the dish or a pod?
- Moths go unseen, but Dun "knows her from the ice" and the Hush draws her picture. Is she invisible or not after Night 2?
- A lampwing landing on Sorrel while he dances: is that a threat or a kindness? It's dropped and never explained.
- The partner's pod moves in and out of the pool several times. Hard to follow.
- Burn ending: she dances "on her last night", but Night 7 was the mountain. Does she get an eighth night?
- How a ball-game brain became a world-eating fungus, through "static", took me two reads.

## 6. What stays with me, and would I buy it?
Umber pressed against a pod, seeing a sleeping face that Tallow can't see while he burns it, and the face going dark. Yes, I'd buy it: a moth too small to be noticed is a great hook, and Dun switching sides got me. I'd worry it's relentlessly sad, though.
