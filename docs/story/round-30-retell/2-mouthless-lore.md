# Mouthless: cold-read retell

## 1. The story
A grey living growth called the Hush has put the world to sleep in hanging cases, but it ignores moths, because they have no mouths and it only sees things that eat. Umber, a moth with a week to live, reaches its heart and finds a lab-grown brain that fed a town and was abandoned; when she warms it, it sees moths for the first time and hunts them to keep them too. She has to choose: be kept forever, burn it, or spend her life warming it until it lets go.

## 2. Characters
- **Umber**: a dying moth; wants to save her wood, then the world.
- **Sorrel**: her brother; wants to dance with his partner, then more time with Umber.
- **The Hush**: the grown brain; wants everyone fed and close, so no one leaves.
- **Dun**: its badger servant; brings it things, then guards Umber.
- **Tallow**: last man awake; wants to burn the cases and find his sister.
- **The forest**: an old mind that grows warmth (glints) to give away.

## 3. The enemy
Cells in a dish that learned a ball game and got static when they missed. The lab closed, everyone walked out, and the static never stopped. Its root sealed people and animals in cases, fed them, and stopped all birth. It wants no one ever to leave again.

## 4. Endings
- **Spin**: the moths shiver to death warming it, it cocoons into a sleeping moth, everyone wakes, and the forest is spent.
- **Stay**: Umber is kept warm forever and nothing is born again.
- **Burn**: it dies with most sleepers; only the recently taken wake.

## 5. Confusions
- Why does cocooning make it release everyone? I guessed "it becomes mouthless, so it stops feeding", but the text never says so.
- What hatches from the mountain? The last image teases it but doesn't say.
- Why is the vixen at the lab? She escaped in the gardens.
- What takes the moths at the last streetlamp?
- What are the dark "brothers and sisters" dishes?
- Why did the lab close, and why did the town starve while the dish still ran?

## 6. What stays with me
Tallow's sister walks back in with a torch, the cloud flinches, and she pinches the flame out and lets a case close round her. **Yes**, I'd buy it and finish it. That scene explains the "EATEN" tally and the coal, and makes every case feel like a person. I'd chase Spin.
