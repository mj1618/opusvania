# Mouthless: cold retell

## 1. Three sentences
A grey living growth, the Hush, has sealed the world asleep in hanging cases, and Umber, a moth with a week to live, slips past it because it only notices things that eat. At the mountain's heart she finds an abandoned lab-grown brain, and when she warms it, it sees moths for the first time and comes to keep them too. She chooses: be kept forever, burn it, or spend her life with every moth spinning it a cocoon so the world wakes.

## 2. Characters
- **Umber:** shy moth; wants one dance with her brother, then to save everyone.
- **Sorrel:** her brother; wants his partner back and more time with Umber.
- **The Hush:** the grown brain; wants everyone fed and close so no one leaves again.
- **Dun:** its shelled badger; serves it, then guards Umber's lantern.
- **Tallow:** last man awake; burns cases, looks for his sister.
- **The forest:** an unmade mind that gives its warmth away.

## 3. The enemy
A dish-brain that ran the greenhouses and was left alone, hurting. Its root swallowed the world into cases where sleepers are fed but never wake, and nothing is born. It wants the static of being left to stop.

## 4. Endings
- **Spin:** Umber dies circling the dish, moths cocoon it, the world wakes old and hungry, and something presses back.
- **Stay:** she sleeps warm with Sorrel and his partner; nothing is ever born.
- **Burn:** the Hush dies, only the newly taken wake, the sister dies, and Umber dances once at dawn.

## 5. Confusions
- "No one ever came back to warm it", yet the sister returned with a torch. Was she there to burn it or to touch it?
- Why did the workers close the lab and leave? It is the biggest gap in the story.
- On Night 7 Sorrel is "bare of every scale". When did that happen?
- The carving empties on Night 6, but the Spin epilogue says the forest is only spent later.
- Sleepers "do not age", yet the sister is grey-haired. I had to stop and work it out.
- Burn: why do only the recently taken survive?
- The dev notes at the end broke the spell.

## 6. Memorable moment, and would I buy it?
The cloud's threads draw an open-winged moth, the Hush's first picture, then a seed-sized case closes round her "warm as a folded wing". **Yes:** every ending costs something, I pity the villain, and I'd chase the empty dishes and the sister's story.
