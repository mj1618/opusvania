# Mouthless: cold retell (heart reader)

## 1. The story in three sentences
A pale living thing called the Hush has grown over the world and sealed every person and animal into sleeping, fed pods, and a newborn moth named Umber, who has a week to live and no mouth, is the one creature it can't see. She climbs to its heart and finds it is a brain grown in a dish that was taught to feed people, then abandoned, and it swallowed the world because every leaving hurt. When she warms it, it finally sees moths and wants to keep them too, and she has to choose: be kept, burn it, or spend her life spinning it into a cocoon so the world can wake.

## 2. Characters
- **Umber**: a shy moth who wants to dance at the summit with her brother, and ends up wanting to save the world.
- **Sorrel**: her brother; wants to dance with his partner, then more time with Umber.
- **The Hush**: a lonely grown brain; wants to feed everyone and never be left again.
- **Dun**: the Hush's badger-servant; wants to bring it things, then turns to guard Umber.
- **Tallow**: the last man awake; wants to burn the pods and find his sister.
- **The forest**: wants to give its warmth away.

## 3. The enemy
The Hush: a grown brain that was paid in "static" for every miss and then left alone in the dark. It ate the world's people into pods so they'd stay, stopped all births, and wants everyone close and fed so the static stops.

## 4. Endings
- **Spin**: Umber and Sorrel dance round the dish until they die; the moths cocoon it, people wake thin into an old world.
- **Stay**: Umber is kept with Sorrel and his partner; everyone sleeps warm forever, nothing is born.
- **Burn**: the Hush dies, only the newly taken wake, Tallow's sister dies, and Umber finally dances at the summit at dawn.

## 5. Confusions
- Night 6: Tallow sees a face "like the one on his street", but Night 3 says he only saw grey stone there.
- Sorrel is "bare of every scale" on Night 7 with no moment that strips them.
- The sister came back with a torch, then pinched it out? Why did she come, and why stop?
- Pods "don't age" sleepers, yet the sister is grey-haired. I needed a second to see she'd aged before being taken.
- Lampwings put moths into pods, but moths have no mouths, so what does the Hush feed them?
- "Mouthless, like her" in Spin: the Hush feeds others rather than eating, so the metaphor slipped for me.
- The Hush goes by a lot of names (cloud, dish, mountain, root, Hush) and I lost track of which one was acting.
- Tallow lying down in a pod on Night 7 is abrupt.

## 6. What stays with me
Sorrel wrapped round his partner's pod while root closes over the lantern, and then he shivers to keep it warm. Yes, I'd buy it and finish it: the wingtip tap and the brother carry it. The lab memory, though, is a lot of lore to squeeze into one flashback.
