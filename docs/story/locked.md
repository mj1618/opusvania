# WING: THE KEPT WORLD: locked story decisions

These are frozen. Story agents refine within them and may only PROPOSE changes (in a "Proposed changes to locked
decisions" note); the orchestrator decides on proposals (the owner asked not to be consulted on every decision). Locked 2026-10-14 from round 18.

## Spine
1. A living thing the moths call the Mouth has swallowed the world into its roots. It never noticed moths, because it
   only sees mouths.
2. Umber, a moth with no mouth, slips through it to its heart and finds a brain grown by people and left to starve,
   feeding the world and never fed.
3. When she feeds it, it sees her kind and comes for them, and she must choose: be kept, burn it, or spend her life so
   it lets go.

**Central question:** kept safe forever, or spent so something new can be born?

## Anchor and presentation
- A mind grown from living brain cells took over the world; on screen and active in every act, at world scale.
- Mythic on the surface (the moths' name for it, wordless first hour); the grown-brain truth is found in play and lands
  at the midpoint.
- It sees only what eats; moths have no mouth, so it dismissed them. That is how Umber gets in.

## Cast (no new principal characters)
Umber (no mouth, a week to live; flaw: she hides) · Sorrel, her brother · the Mouth · Dun, its oldest burrback ·
Tallow, the last man awake, and his sister who made it (seen, not a principal) · the forest.

## Fixed beats
- **Midpoint (Night 4):** in the lab at the mountain's heart she finds it has been feeding the world, not eating it; it
  has never been given anything; its maker came back. Umber gives it every glint she carries; it sees her, and through
  her every moth.
- **Lowest point (Night 5):** the Grove overrun; the Mouth now sees her; Sorrel leaves; Dun refuses it.
- **Endings:** Spin (true: every moth warms it until it lets go; the world wakes old and hungry; both moths die), Stay
  (kept together forever; nothing born again), Burn (it dies in a night; most sleepers die with it).

## Constraints
- Wordless or nearly. Audio is now allowed if generated in code (see ../audio-direction.md); design big beats to be sound-led.
- Every beat must be drawable in this engine (code-drawn, few new characters).

## The three big images (owner's picks, 2026-10-14)
- **Prologue opening: A, "The breath."** A small pale light breathing in the dark; a warm blur (the maker's palm, unreadable) rests on it, lifts away, and the light stutters into grey grain. Night 4 widens this exact frame.
- **Night 4 turning moment: B, "The cloud draws a moth."** Amber enters the dish for the first time, the static stops, its threads draw a moth (the first picture it has ever made); then every root turns to her; end on one lampwing's lamp settling on Sorrel in the Grove.
- **Final image: C, "The hand, returned."** The maker, old, lays her palm on the mountain's silk, framed like the prologue's blur; something presses back from inside; she doesn't lift her hand.

## Names (owner, 2026-10-14)
- **Game title: Wing: The Kept World** (owner, 2026-10-14: "Wing" with a subtitle, like Ori). The subtitle names the place, as "the Blind Forest" does, and the central question (kept safe forever, or spent?); it also makes the title searchable.
- **Hero: Umber** (renamed from Vesper, then from Eri, which sounded too close to Ori; 2026-10-14). The umbers are a real family of dusk moths (the mottled umber, the dark umber), and umber is an earth colour: she and her brother Sorrel are both named for warm browns. Code keeps the internal id `vesper`.

## De-mouthing (orchestrator decision, 2026-10-14, after the owner asked if it was "a bit too mouthy")
- The AI's mythic name is **the Hush** (the moths' name: it put the world to sleep, it breathes, it is silent). Its human
  name, found at the midpoint on the lab sign, is **HEARTH** ("No one goes hungry"). Never "the Mouth".
- Umber having no mouth is the ONE mouth idea: shown (she raises a glint to her face and can do nothing with it), named
  at most once in item text. All her kind are mouthless.
- The rule is phrased "it only notices things that need feeding"; its ground openings are "hollows", not mouths.
- The game is titled **Wing: The Kept World** (owner rejected "Mouthless"; Hushwing superseded). Files keep the slug `mouthless`.

## Distinctness guardrails (from uniqueness.md, 2026-10-14)
- Moths never worship light; they carry and give it. The Hush is pale bone and breath: never orange glow, never
  infected eyes (Hollow Knight's Radiance/infection).
- The forest is one region; light is given away, not collected for power; the hero does not become a tree (Ori).
- Sleepers are fed by a starving made thing that thinks it cares: never "humans as fuel/batteries" (Nine Sols, The Matrix).
- Avoid a bare "light N things" structure: the lanterns are given to, and the second act's goal is a person.
- The game must PLAY like giving: carrying light makes you seen; empty-handed you slip through; Offer calms bosses;
  progression is paid by giving away.
