# Naming: title and franchise candidates

Date: 2026-09-28. Input: `PLAN.md` §2 and `docs/concepts/raw-L1-p4.md` Concept A (pitched as DISTRAINT, which is a 2015 game and can't be used). Working title: **Tallage**.

Brief: a title for the game (ideally the franchise) that is original, memorable, searchable, pronounceable, fits the fiction (Kid Tallow, disgraced featherweight turned bailiff/redistrainer; sounds are title deeds; seize/levy; noise colours; feverish boomtown; weight classes), and is not already used by a notable game, film, band or big brand.

Seeds used as springboards, alongside the fiction:
- A: "Michaela Šojdrová", "Pacific Christian Hospital", "List of dog breeds originating from Scotland"; hoovey, semisecrecy, ligurition, glossopetra, studder, holard.
- B: "Kassian Bogatyrets", "adjustable", "rheumatiz"; niobite, lewdly, vasotripsy, umbonal, moonseed, anabranch.

## 1. Seed associations (what the seeds gave us)

- **glossopetra**, "tongue stone": fossil shark teeth once believed to be petrified tongues, worn as amulets against poison. A *sound turned to stone* is literally what Levy does. Gives the invented word **Tonguestone**, and a good in-game noun for a carried sound.
- **anabranch**: a river channel that leaves the main stream and rejoins it downstream. That is Kid Tallow's arc (enforcer who returns what was taken) and the redistrain loop. Better as a studio or publisher name than a title.
- **holard** (total soil water) → "holler'd", a hoarded holler. **hoovey** (bloat) → a bloated boom. **studder** → stutter + shudder. **umbonal** → umbo, the boss of a shield. **ligurition** (gluttony) → ligure, a biblical gemstone. **niobite** → Niobe, who wept until she turned to stone.
- **rheumatiz** → the whole city rattles and creaks; "rattle" is a pink-noise sound. **adjustable** → an adjustable scale, an insurance adjuster. **vasotripsy** (crushing a vessel) → "tripsy", crushing.
- **Pacific Christian Hospital** → a fever ward; the Receivership as an isolation ward. **Scottish dog breeds** (Skye, Cairn, Gordon, Border, Dandie Dinmont) → names for the bark-seized dogs and Tallow's corner-man's dog, not titles. **Šojdrová**, **Bogatyrets**, **Kassian** → NPC and rival names (a "Sojdra" broker; "Kassian" as the Auctioneer's given name).
- **semisecrecy** → half-hush, the leaky bag in the Static Flats. **moonseed**, **lewdly** → nothing usable.

## 4. Final three

### 1. TALLAGE

**Rationale.** It names the world, so it names the franchise: sequels and spin-offs stay in Tallage the way *Hades* stays in Hades. The word is a tax levied by a lord on tenants, which is the game's entire economy (seize, levy, distrain) folded into one archaic noun that nobody else in games is using. Two syllables, TAL-ij, easy to say and spell after hearing it once. Reads well small on a Steam capsule. It also keeps the project's existing doc trail intact.

**Weaknesses.** Dry on first contact; it doesn't carry the boxing or the fever. Could be misheard as "tillage" or "tally". It needs the tagline and key art to bring the heat ("Every sound is a debt.").

**Conflicts found.** None. No game on Steam, itch.io or IGDB; no film, band or brand. Web search returns only dictionary and Wikipedia entries, which a store page will outrank. tallage.com, tallage.io and tallagegame.com have no DNS (verify at a registrar).

**Wordmark concept.** `T A L L A G E` in heavy slab-serif capitals, stamped rather than printed: red ledger ink, slightly over-inked at the edges, the baseline a hair off-level as if hand-stamped. The two `L`s are drawn as tally strokes and a thin red diagonal strikes through them, the ledger mark for a seized lot. On the title screen the outline hums (the same vibrating-edge shader as an in-world sound source); pressing Seize mutes it to a dashed ghost outline before the game starts.

### 2. TONGUESTONE

**Rationale.** The most distinctive brand of the three. A tongue stone is a fossil tooth people believed was a petrified tongue: a sound made solid, which is exactly what Levy produces. It gives the fiction a missing noun: the thing in your bag is a *tonguestone*, the Hum Registry files title in tonguestones, and the belt Kid Tallow wants back is set with one. One compound word, evocative and slightly grotesque in the *Hollow Knight* / *Blasphemous* register, and near-unique in search. Franchise-able as a series name with subtitles.

**Weaknesses.** It says "sound turned to stone" but not "city", "boxer" or "bailiff"; it needs the subtitle or key art for that. It is one letter away from looking like "Tongue Stone" as two words, so the wordmark has to fuse them. Requires the small lore addition above, which is cheap.

**Conflicts found.** Tonguestone Property Developments, a small UK property firm, owns tonguestone.com. No game on Steam, itch.io or IGDB; no film, band or book. tonguestonegame.com has no DNS. A property developer in another sector is not a trademark barrier for an entertainment title, but the .com is gone.

**Wordmark concept.** One word, tall condensed sans, letters cut rather than set. Split at the seam: `TONGUE` on the left drawn as a dashed, vibrating ghost outline (a sound, not yet solid); `STONE` on the right solid, chipped, with the `O` a rough pebble. Read left to right it *is* the mechanic: hum becomes stone. Colour: brown-noise umber on the solid half, the outline in pink.

### 3. KID TALLOW

**Rationale.** The character-name title in the boxing tradition (Kid Chocolate, Kid Galahad), which is also a game tradition (Kid Icarus, Kid Chameleon), so it lands instantly as "a fighter, and a game". Warm and human where the other two are nouns; puts the protagonist and her tone on the box, which helps the redistrainer-to-redistributor arc read as a person's story rather than a bureaucracy's. Tallow (rendered fat, candle stuff) fits a boomtown of furnaces and gyms.

**Weaknesses.** The weakest for search: "kid tallow" currently returns beef-tallow baby skincare, a live trend, so early discoverability suffers. Ties the franchise to one protagonist. The "Kid ___" formula is familiar enough to feel borrowed.

**Conflicts found.** No game titled "Tallow" or "Kid Tallow" on Steam, itch.io or IGDB (only an itch.io developer account "Tallow Wren"). Songs named "Tallow" exist but none notable. kidtallow.com has no DNS.

**Wordmark concept.** Fight-bill typography. `KID` small, in a circular badge top-left like a promoter's seal; `TALLOW` huge in woodblock grotesque, ink-starved at the edges as if pulled from a worn poster block. Under it, a thin ribbon: `FEATHERWEIGHT · TALLAGE CIRCUIT`, struck through in red and re-lettered `BAILIFF` beside it. The whole lock-up sits slightly askew, pasted over an older poster.

### Reserve: REPLEVIN

The legal action to recover goods wrongfully taken, which is the plot in one word and the natural successor to DISTRAINT in register. Mostly clear (a tiny itch.io visual novel "Replevin Punks"; replevin.com is a law firm; search is legal dictionaries). Ranked fourth because its pronunciation (ruh-PLEV-in) isn't obvious from the spelling and it repeats the DISTRAINT strategy of a bare legal term, which reads cold. Best use: a subtitle, **Tallage: Replevin**, or a chapter title.

## 5. Recommendation

Keep **TALLAGE** as the title and franchise name; adopt **tonguestone** as the in-game noun for a carried sound (and keep TONGUESTONE in reserve as a sequel or spin-off title); use **Kid Tallow** as the character brand on posters and the store page's first line. Candidate subtitles, in order: *Tallage: Replevin*, *Tallage: Make Weight*.

Before committing: registrar check on tallage.com / .io, a trademark search in the relevant classes (9 and 41), and social-handle checks for "tallage" and "tallagegame".
