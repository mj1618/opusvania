# AI critics: use them as lint, not judges

Story rounds 13–31 scored 8–9/10 "at the bar" from AI critic panels while Matt found the story and prologue "bad and
random". The critics graded prose against their own brief (Goodhart), imagined every beat executed at Ori quality, and
shared the writer's taste for callbacks and symmetry. When a critic was shown the rendered frames instead of prose, it
said "not at the bar".

- Ask critics narrow, checkable questions: "what can't you see in this frame?", "what would you cut?", "retell this
  recording in one sentence". Don't ask "is it at the bar?" and don't treat 1–10 scores as signal.
- Judge the built thing (frames, clips, play), never the doc. Matt's reaction is the only score.
- A generate→review loop must be allowed to cut. One that may only add turns every note into a new beat.
