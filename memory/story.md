# The story (locked 2026-10-17)

**Title: Lampfall** (chosen 2026-10-17. Searched clear on Steam, Google Play, the App Store, itch and the web. Runners-up:
Truemoon, Lampblind, Moonblind, Mothward. Moonward was taken: a Play Store game and a Critical Role series. Nearby games to set ourselves apart from:
The Moth and the Moon, Mothkeep. Avoid moth-and-moon titles). The hero is **Wing**, the dark moth with glowing eyes (code id `vesper`). Matt chose this story on
2026-10-17 from ten pitches. It merges "Moonward" (false lights) with "The Long Flight" (migration), and the firefly
answers who lit the lamps. The old "Kept World" story and prologue were deleted the same day; git history has them.
Why the story is this light: `docs/research/story-direction.md`. Reference games per design problem: `reference-games.md`.

## In one line
Every spring the moths fly by the moon to a cave in the mountains to sleep. This year false lights hang along the
way, and the swarm breaks apart circling them. Wing flies by the real moon to bring her kind home.

## The real biology it stands on
- Bogong moths migrate up to 1,000 km to the same alpine caves every year, steering by the night sky.
  City lights pull them off course; swarms have circled the floodlights of Parliament House in Canberra.
- Moths steer by keeping the moon's light above their backs. A lamp close by breaks this, and they spiral in.
- Fireflies flash to find a mate, and they are disappearing because of light pollution.

## Who lit the lamps: the last firefly
It is the last of its kind. It hangs every glowing thing it can find along the moths' road, signalling for another
firefly to answer. It doesn't know the lights trap moths. It lives in the moths' cave, which its collection has made the
brightest place in the world. It is not evil; it is lonely. Rules that make this work:
1. **The fighting is against things that really are dangerous.** Predators gather wherever moths circle: spiders, the
   mantis, bats, toads. The firefly is the cause, not the everyday enemy.
2. **It is still a threat.** Every lamp she puts out is its hope going dark, so it fights for its lights. The final
   fight is hard; the mercy is in the ending, not the difficulty.
3. **Its reason comes out late.** Early on it's a light that flees and relights lamps behind her (as with Kuro in Ori).
4. **The stakes stay real.** Moths really die at the lamps.

## How it plays
- **Lamps pull on her like gravity.** First they're traps to fight free of, then slingshots to whip around (like
  Ori's Bash), then things she can snuff out. The dark is safe; light is danger and speed.
- **Every lamp she puts out frees a cloud of moths.** They fly with her for a while, then go on toward the mountain.
  Lamps she can't reach yet are the reason to come back with new abilities. The count of freed moths is completion.
- **The goal is in view.** The mountain under the moon is seen at vistas (canopy gaps, clifftops, a camera lift each
  time a lamp goes out) and gets closer each region. Underground, the freed moths flying off show the way.
- **Regions, in order (all natural art):** the wood (the Grove, the demo level), the valley of lamps, the marsh, the
  pass, and the cave.

## Opening
Baseline: she hatches on a branch. In front of her, a lamp in the valley with moths circling it. Above it, the
moon over the mountain. Control within 10 seconds, title over the moon. Upgrade to try when we get there: she's
the last to hatch, chases the swarm streaming past the moon, sees it break apart over the valley lamps, gets caught by one and
beats free.

## Bosses (first ideas)
- The mantis under the brightest lamp (a fast fighter).
- A grove of lamps that pull from every side.
- The bat colony under the bridge.
- The beacon on the pass, a lamp the size of a tree.
- The cave of lights.
- The firefly.

## Ending
The cave blazes with the firefly's collection. She puts out its lights one by one while it fights to keep them.
When the last goes dark, the moths settle to sleep in the black, and every moth she freed comes home beside her. How full the ceiling is
depends on how many she saved. Far off, through the cave mouth, one firefly flash answers. The dark that was safety all
game is home.

## Ground rules
- The story is told by the world, the creatures and what the player does. Keep text minimal (item lines, a title).
- **Try before cutting:** hard parts (the fast mantis, scripted scenes, the firefly's acting) get a real attempt when
  we reach them. Cut them only if that attempt fails, as judged by Matt.
- Judge story work from built frames and play, never from docs. AI critic scores are not the bar (`ai-critics.md`).
- The demo level stays as it is until gameplay work deliberately changes it. Its glints, lanterns, burrbacks and
  lampwings come from the old design and will be reworked toward this story one increment at a time.
